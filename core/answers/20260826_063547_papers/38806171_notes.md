### Study design
The paper is a narrative review and update on heart failure management, not a primary study. It discusses the 2023 ESC focused update of the 2021 HF guidelines, which includes recommendations for SGLT2 inhibitors in HF across the entire left ventricular ejection fraction spectrum. It also references clinical trials such as STEP-HFpEF-DM and STEP-HFpEF, which evaluated semaglutide in HFpEF patients.

### Key findings relevant to the question
- In the STEP-HFpEF-DM and STEP-HFpEF trials, semaglutide 2.4 mg once weekly administered for 1 year decreased body weight and significantly improved quality of life and the 6 min walk distance in obese patients with HFpEF with or without a history of diabetes.

### Limitations stated by the authors
- The paper notes that further data on safety and efficacy, including also hard endpoints, are needed to support the addition of acetazolamide or hydrochlorothiazide to a standard diuretic regimen in patients hospitalized due to acute HF.
