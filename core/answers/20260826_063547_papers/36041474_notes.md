### Study design  
- **Type**: Meta-analysis of five randomized controlled trials (RCTs)  
- **Population**: Patients with heart failure (HF), including those with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF), mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF/HFmrEF)  
- **n**: 21,947 participants across five trials (DELIVER, EMPEROR-Preserved, DAPA-HF, EMPEROR-Reduced, SOLOIST-WHF)  
- **Intervention/Comparator**: SGLT2 inhibitors vs. placebo or standard care  
- **Follow-up**: Varies by trial, but outcomes were assessed from randomization to occurrence of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- In the broader context of five trials (21,947 participants), SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of composite cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure (HR 0.77 [95% CI 0.72–0.82]).  
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduced cardiovascular death (HR 0.87 [0.79–0.95]) and first hospitalisation for heart failure (HR 0.72 [0.67–0.78]).  
- All-cause mortality was reduced (HR 0.92 [0.86–0.99]).  
- These treatment effects were consistently observed in trials of heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF/HFmrEF).  
- Treatment effects on the primary endpoint were generally consistent across 14 subgroups, including ejection fraction.
