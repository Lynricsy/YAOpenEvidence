### Relevance  
3  

### Study design  
- **Type**: Double-blind, randomized controlled trial  
- **Population**: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF), class II-IV heart failure, ejection fraction >40%  
- **n**: 5988 patients (2997 in empagliflozin group, 2991 in placebo group)  
- **Intervention/Comparator**: Empagliflozin (10 mg once daily) vs. placebo, in addition to usual therapy  
- **Follow-up**: Median of 26.2 months  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- The primary outcome (composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure) occurred in **13.8%** of the empagliflozin group vs. **17.1%** of the placebo group (**HR 0.79; 95% CI 0.69–0.90; p < 0.001**).  
- The effect was mainly due to a **lower risk of hospitalization for heart failure** in the empagliflozin group.  
- Total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was **407** in the empagliflozin group vs. **541** in the placebo group (**HR 0.73; 95% CI 0.61–0.88; p < 0.001**).  
- The benefits of empagliflozin were consistent in patients with or without diabetes.
