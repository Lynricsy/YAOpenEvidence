### Study design  
- **Type**: Randomized controlled trial  
- **Population**: Patients with heart failure and left ventricular ejection fraction >40% (HFpEF and HFmrEF)  
- **n**: 6263 patients  
- **Intervention/Comparator**: Dapagliflozin (10 mg once daily) vs. placebo  
- **Follow-up**: Median of 2.3 years  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- The primary outcome (worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death) occurred in **16.4%** of the dapagliflozin group vs. **19.5%** of the placebo group (**HR 0.82; 95% CI 0.73–0.92; p < 0.001**).  
- Worsening heart failure occurred in **11.8%** of the dapagliflozin group vs. **14.5%** of the placebo group (**HR 0.79; 95% CI 0.69–0.91**).  
- Cardiovascular death occurred in **7.4%** of the dapagliflozin group vs. **8.3%** of the placebo group (**HR 0.88; 95% CI 0.74–1.05**).  
- Total events and symptom burden were lower in the dapagliflozin group.  
- Results were consistent across subgroups, including patients with preserved ejection fraction (LVEF ≥60%) and those with or without diabetes.
