### Study design  
- **Type**: Multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized trial  
- **Population**: 100 patients with HFpEF and type 2 diabetes (T2D)  
- **n**: 100 (50 in each group: dapagliflozin 10 mg daily vs. placebo)  
- **Intervention/Comparator**: Dapagliflozin 10 mg daily vs. placebo  
- **Follow-up**: 12 months  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- **Myocardial fibrosis reduction**: Dapagliflozin significantly reduced myocardial fibrosis (mean ΔECV: −3.5% [95% CI −4.2 to −2.8]) compared to placebo (−0.8% [95% CI −1.3 to −0.4]; p < 0.001).  
- **Left ventricular mass index (LVMI)**: Greater reduction in LVMI with dapagliflozin (−8.2 g/m² vs. −2.1 g/m²; p = 0.002).  
- **Glycemic control**: Greater reduction in HbA1c (−1.2% vs. −0.4%; p = 0.01).  
- **Functional capacity**: Improved 6MWT distance (+45 m vs. +10 m; p = 0.01).  
- **NT-proBNP levels**: Significant reduction in dapagliflozin group (−210 pg/mL [95% CI −250 to −170]) vs. placebo (−50 pg/mL [95% CI −120 to +20]; p = 0.008).  
- **Hospitalization rate**: Lower HF hospitalization rate in dapagliflozin group (4%) vs. placebo (12%; p = 0.03).  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- **Sample size and follow-up**: Modest sample size and short follow-up may limit generalizability and statistical power for rare events.  
- **Lack of molecular markers**: No tissue-based or molecular markers (e.g., TGF-β, collagen turnover) were included to confirm antifibrotic mechanisms.  
- **Population heterogeneity**: The cohort consisted largely of Middle Eastern patients, which may limit extrapolation to other populations.  
- **LGE assessment**: No assessment of LGE burden or patterns, which could provide complementary insights into fibrosis characterization.
