### Relevance
3

### Study design  
Type: Systematic review and meta-analysis  
Population: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF)  
n: 16,509 participants across 8 studies  
Intervention/Comparator: SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin, sotogliflozin, ertugliflozin) vs. placebo  
Follow-up: Varies by study (12 weeks to 26.2 months)

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- **Cardiovascular hospitalization**: SGLT2 inhibitors significantly reduced the risk of cardiovascular hospitalization compared to placebo (Mean Difference (MD) = 0.78; 95% CI = 0.72–0.85).  
- **Composite outcome of cardiovascular death or hospitalization**: SGLT2 inhibitors showed a statistically significant reduction in the composite outcome of cardiovascular death or hospitalization (MD = 0.78; 95% CI = 0.72–0.85).  
- **Heart failure hospitalization**: SGLT2 inhibitors significantly reduced the risk of heart failure hospitalization compared to placebo (MD = 0.74; 95% CI = 0.67–0.83).  
- **Cardiovascular death**: No statistically significant reduction in cardiovascular death was observed (MD = 0.92; 95% CI = 0.81–1.03).  
- **All-cause death**: No statistically significant reduction in all-cause death was observed (MD = 0.97; 95% CI = 0.89–1.06).  
- **Effect across subgroups**: The benefits of SGLT2 inhibitors were consistent across subgroups including eGFR, systolic blood pressure, diabetes status, sex, BMI, and NT-proBNP, except for patients with NYHA class III/IV and Black race, where results were heterogeneous or less favorable.  
- **Kidney outcomes**: SGLT2 inhibitors showed a decreased risk of kidney injury and progression of nephropathy.

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The results for cardiovascular death and all-cause death were not statistically significant.  
- The study had heterogeneity in results for patients with NYHA class III/IV and Black race.  
- The included studies had varying follow-up durations and outcomes.  
- More studies are needed for longer durations, reporting adverse events, effects on exercise tolerance, and other secondary outcomes.
