### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction (HFmrEF or HFpEF)  
- Inclusion criteria: Age ≥40 years, symptomatic HF (NYHA II-IV), treatment with a diuretic for >30 days before randomization, LVEF ≥40% with structural heart disease (left atrial enlargement or left ventricular hypertrophy), elevated natriuretic peptide levels (NT-proBNP >300 pg/mL or BNP >100 pg/mL for sinus rhythm; NT-proBNP >900 pg/mL or BNP >300 pg/mL for atrial fibrillation)  
- Exclusion criteria: eGFR <25 mL/min/1.73 m², serum/plasma potassium >5.0 mEq/L, symptomatic hypotension with mean systolic blood pressure <90 mm Hg  
- Age/sex: Mean age 72.0 (9.6) years; 45.5% women  
- Setting: Prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, event-driven trial across 37 countries  
- Sample size: 6001 patients (3003 finerenone, 2998 placebo)  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Finerenone (10 mg once daily for eGFR ≤60 mL/min/1.73 m², up to 20 mg once daily; 20 mg once daily for eGFR >60 mL/min/1.73 m², up to 40 mg once daily)  
- Duration: Not explicitly stated, but follow-up period was 32 (23-37) months  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo  
- Dose & duration: Placebo administered in a 1:1 ratio with finerenone  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Composite of first HF hospitalization or cardiovascular (CV) death  
- Secondary outcomes: CV death, all-cause death, total HF events (first and recurrent), and total HF events alone  
- Outcomes with exact numbers:  
  - For the composite outcome of first HF hospitalization or CV death:  
    - Incidence rate in lowest risk quintile: 2.2 (95% CI, 1.7-2.8) per 100 patient-years  
    - Incidence rate in highest risk quintile: 25.0 (95% CI, 23.0-27.3) per 100 patient-years  
    - HR for finerenone vs. placebo: 0.93 (95% CI, 0.58-1.49) to 0.88 (95% CI, 0.74-1.05) across quintiles  
    - Absolute risk reduction: 3.7 per 1000 person-years in lowest risk quintile vs. 43.0 per 1000 person-years in highest risk quintile  
  - For CV death: HR of 13.47 (95% CI, 8.79-20.64) between lowest and highest risk quintiles  
  - For all-cause death: HR of 13.47 (95% CI, 8.79-20.64) between lowest and highest risk quintiles  
- Follow-up time: 32 (23-37) months  
- Adverse events:  
  - Finerenone group had higher incidences of hypotension, elevated creatinine, and hyperkalemia compared to placebo  
  - Lower incidence of hypokalemia in finerenone group  
  - Differences in adverse events were consistent across risk quintiles  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, event-driven trial  
- Blinding: Double-blind  
- Randomization: 1:1 ratio to finerenone or placebo  
- Number of centres: 653 participating institutions in 37 countries  
- Registration: Ethics committees approved the protocol  
- Follow-up duration: 32 (23-37) months  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- The EMPEROR-Preserved risk model demonstrated good discrimination and generally preserved calibration across mortality outcomes in patients with HFpEF (¶34: "One of the principal findings of the present study is that the EMPEROR-Preserved risk model demonstrated robust performance in the FINEARTS-HF trial, showing good discrimination and generally preserved calibration except within the highest-risk quintile for first HF hospitalization or CV death.")  
- The relative risk reduction with finerenone was consistent across the full spectrum of baseline risk, whereas the absolute risk reduction was greater among patients at higher risk (¶40: "Another important finding of this study is that the relative risk reduction with finerenone was consistent across the full spectrum of baseline risk, whereas the absolute risk reduction was greater among patients at higher risk.")  
- Absolute risk reductions for the composite outcome of first HF hospitalization or CV death were 3.7 per 1000 person-years in the lowest risk quintile and 43.0 per 1000 person-years in the highest risk quintile (¶29: "Applying the overall relative risk reduction for the primary composite outcome... gave an absolute reduction in risk of 3.7 per 1000 person-years... these numbers were 43.0 per 1000 person-years...")  
- Finerenone showed consistent relative risk reduction across all quintiles of the EMPEROR-Preserved risk score (¶29: "For the composite outcome of first HF hospitalization or CV death, the relative effect of finerenone was consistent across quintiles of the EMPEROR-Preserved risk score...")  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Residual confounding or unmeasured heterogeneity across subgroups cannot be excluded (¶43: "First, the possibility of residual confounding or unmeasured heterogeneity across subgroups cannot be excluded.")  
- Subgroup interaction analyses were underpowered (¶43: "Furthermore, the subgroup interaction analyses were underpowered, raising the possibility that true effect modification may exist.")  
- Generalizability may be limited due to strict inclusion/exclusion criteria (¶43: "Second, the study patients were derived from a randomized clinical trial with stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria. This selective enrollment may limit the generalizability of our findings to the broader population of patients with HFmrEF and HFpEF.")  
- One laboratory variable (albumin) was imputed using median values from the EMPEROR-Preserved trial, which may affect calibration and predictive performance (¶43: "Finally, one laboratory variable required for the EMPEROR-Preserved risk score was unavailable in FINEARTS-HF and was imputed using the median value from EMPEROR-Preserved. This approach may reduce variability and attenuate true associations, potentially affecting calibration and predictive performance.")
