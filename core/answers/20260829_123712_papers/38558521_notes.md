### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Hospitalization for heart failure (HHF) or cardiovascular (CV) death  
  - Treatment effect of empagliflozin versus placebo was consistent across BMI categories (p trend = 0.19)  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - Total HHF (p trend = 0.19)  
  - CV death (p trend = 0.20)  
  - Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) slope: Slower declines with empagliflozin regardless of BMI (range 1.12-1.71 ml/min/1.73 m² relative to placebo, p trend = 0.85 for interaction)  
  - Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire clinical summary score (KCCQ-CSS): Trend toward greater absolute benefit at higher BMIs (p = 0.08)  
- Weight change: Difference between empagliflozin and placebo was -0.59, -1.48, -1.54, -0.87, and -2.67 kg in the lowest to highest BMI categories (p trend = 0.016 for interaction)  
- Follow-up time: 32 weeks for KCCQ-CSS benefit  
- Adverse events: Not explicitly stated
