### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF)  
- Inclusion criteria: Patients with preserved ejection fraction (>40% ejection fraction)  
- Exclusion criteria: Patients with reduced ejection fraction (<40%), studies not involving SGLT2 inhibitors, non-randomized observational trials, case reports, case series, cohort studies, systematic reviews, and animal studies  
- Age: Adult patients (>18 years)  
- Setting: Not explicitly stated  
- Sample size: 16,509 participants across 8 studies  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin, sotogliflozin, ertugliflozin)  
- Dose: Not explicitly stated  
- Frequency: Not explicitly stated  
- Route: Not explicitly stated  
- Duration: Not explicitly stated  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo  
- Dose & Duration: Not explicitly stated  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes:  
  - Cardiovascular death or hospitalization for HF: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (Mean Difference (MD) = 0.78; 95% CI = (0.72–0.85)) (¶30: "Pooled studies analysis showed statistically significant results between SGLT2i group and placebo group favoring SGLT2i group (Mean Difference (MD) = 0.78; 95% CI = (0.72–0.85))")  
  - HF hospitalization: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (MD = 0.74; 95% CI = (0.67–0.83)) (¶33: "Pooled studies analysis showed statistically significant results between the SGLT2i group and the placebo group favoring the SGLT2i group (MD = 0.74; 95% CI = (0.67–0.83))")  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - Cardiovascular death: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (MD = 0.92; 95% CI = (0.81–1.03)) (¶32: "Pooled studies analysis showed statistically insignificant results between the SGLT2i group and the placebo group (MD = 0.92; 95% CI = (0.81–1.03))")  
  - All-cause death: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (MD = 0.97; 95% CI = (0.89–1.06)) (¶34: "Pooled studies analysis showed statistically insignificant results between the SGLT2i group and the placebo group (MD = 0.97; 95% CI = (0.89–1.06))")  
  - Death from any cause: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (MD = 0.97; 95% CI = (0.88–1.06)) (¶35: "Pooled studies analysis showed statistically insignificant results between the SGLT2i group and the placebo group (MD = 0.97; 95% CI = (0.88–1.06))")  
- Follow-up time: Not explicitly stated  
- Adverse events: Not explicitly stated

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- SGLT2 inhibitors were associated with a decreased risk of cardiovascular hospitalization in HFpEF patients (¶6: "different SGLT2 inhibitors lead to a decreased risk of cardiovascular hospitalization and kidney injury")  
- SGLT2 inhibitors showed a decreased risk of cardiovascular hospitalization but not death due to cardiovascular causes or other causes (¶6: "Our meta-analysis showed a decreased risk of cardiovascular hospitalization but not death due to cardiovascular causes or other causes")  
- The results were consistent across various subgroups, including baseline eGFR, systolic blood pressure, atrial fibrillation or flutter, diabetes mellitus, sex, body mass index, and NT-proBNP (¶36: "The effect of SGLT2i on the incidence of primary outcome events was generally consistent across prespecified subgroups...")  
- SGLT2 inhibitors were associated with a decreased risk of renal failure (¶28: "SCORED trial 29 examined the drug sotagliflozin and demonstrated a decreased rate of renal failure in patients treated with the drug")  
- SGLT2 inhibitors were associated with a decreased risk of hospitalization for HF in patients with HFpEF (¶33: "Pooled studies analysis showed statistically significant results between the SGLT2i group and the placebo group favoring the SGLT2i group (MD = 0.74; 95% CI = (0.67–0.83))")  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Due to the small number of studies and short duration of the systematic review and meta-analysis, more research is required (¶73: "Due to the small number of studies and short duration of our systematic review and meta-analysis, more research is required to ass")  
- The results for patients with NYHA class III and IV and non-white races were heterogeneous and non-conclusive (¶36: "However, the result was heterogeneous in patients with NYHA class III and IV and non-white races, with increased risk in black patients")  
- The effect of SGLT2 inhibitors on all-cause death and cardiovascular death was not significant (¶29: "Studies examined the influence of different variables on SGLT2 inhibitor effectiveness in HFpEF patients. HF hospitalization and cardiovascular death were both reduced by SGLT2i; however, all-cause and cardiovascular death were not")
