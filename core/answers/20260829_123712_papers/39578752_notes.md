### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes:

  - Reduction in left ventricular end-diastolic volume (LVEDV): MD = -18.05 mL (95% CI=[-25.3, -10.80], P < 0.0001) (¶25: "MD=-18.05 mL, 95% CI=[-25.3, -10.80]; P < 0.0001")  
  - Reduction in left ventricular end-systolic volume (LVESV): MD = -16.05 mL (95% CI=[-27.59, -5.50], P = 0.006) (¶25: "MD=-16.05 mL, 95% CI=[-27.59, -5.50]; P = 0.006")

- Secondary outcomes:
