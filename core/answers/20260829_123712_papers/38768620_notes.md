### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes:  
  - First hospitalisation for heart failure: HR 0.71 (95% CI 0.67-0.77) in patients with heart failure (¶3: "Compared with placebo, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of first hospitalisation for heart failure by 29% in patients with heart failure (hazard ratio [HR] 0·71 [95% CI 0·67-0·77])")  
  - Cardiovascular death: HR 0.86 (95% CI 0.79-0.93) in patients with heart failure (¶3: "SGLT2 inhibitors reduced cardiovascular death by 14% in patients with heart failure (HR 0·86 [95% CI 0·79-0·93])")  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - First hospitalisation for heart failure in HFpEF: 26% reduction (no effect on cardiovascular death) (¶4: "heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (26% reduction in first hospitalisation for heart failure; no effect on cardiovascular death).")  
- Not reported: Absolute event rates, mean differences, follow-up time, adverse events.

- Registered with PROSPERO, CRD42024513836 (¶2: "This study is registered with PROSPERO, CRD42024513836.")  
- Not reported: Blinding, randomization, number of centres, follow-up duration.

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of first hospitalisation for heart failure by 26% in patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) (¶4: "heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (26% reduction in first hospitalisation for heart failure; no effect on cardiovascular death).")  
- No significant effect on cardiovascular death in patients with HFpEF (¶4: "heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (26% reduction in first hospitalisation for heart failure; no effect on cardiovascular death).")
