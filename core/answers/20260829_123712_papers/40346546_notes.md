### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) and heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) (¶1)  
- Inclusion criteria: Stable HF according to NYHA functional class II–III criteria, LV ejection fraction ≥ 50%, and increased LV filling pressure at rest and/or during exercise (determined by echocardiography) (¶2)  
- Exclusion criteria: Previous SGLT2 inhibitor therapy, type 1 diabetes, uncontrolled hyperglycemia, severe hypoglycemia, eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m², liver disease, significant untreated coronary artery stenoses, inability to complete exercise, inadequate acoustic windows, chronic atrial flutter/fibrillation, left bundle branch block, significant left-sided structural valve disease, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, infiltrative or inflammatory myocardial diseases, pericardial disease, or noncardiac conditions precluding participation (¶16)  
- Age: ≥ 40 years (¶15)  
- Sex: 63% women (¶33)  
- Setting: Single-center trial (¶2)  
- Sample size: 70 patients (n = 35 per arm) (¶17)

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Empagliflozin (10 mg/day) for 6 months (¶2)  
- Duration: 6 months (¶2)

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Conventional antidiabetic therapy other than SGLT2 inhibitors (n = 35) (¶18)  
- Duration: 6 months (¶2)

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Change in 6-minute walk test distance (6MWD):  
  - Empagliflozin group: +16 m (95% CI 8–24)  
  - Control group: +3 m (95% CI –6–12)  
  - Between-group difference: P = 0.033 (¶38)  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - LA volume index:  
    - Empagliflozin group: –2.9 mL/m² (95% CI –4.2 to –1.6)  
    - Control group: +0.4 mL/m² (95% CI –0.9 to 1.7)  
    - Between-group difference: P < 0.001 (¶42)  
  - E/e′ ratio (at rest):  
    - Empagliflozin group: –1.4 (95% CI –2.0 to –1.0)  
    - Control group: +0.3 (95% CI –0.2 to 0.9)  
    - Between-group difference: P < 0.001 (¶42)  
  - Pulmonary artery systolic pressure (PASP):  
    - Empagliflozin group: –4.2 mm Hg (95% CI –7.0 to –1.5)  
    - Control group: +1.1 mm Hg (95% CI –1.1 to 3.4)  
    - Between-group difference: P = 0.003 (¶42)  
  - Tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion (TAPSE):  
    - Empagliflozin group: +0.12 cm (95% CI 0.03–0.20)  
    - Control group: –0.10 cm (95% CI –0.17 to –0.03)  
    - Between-group difference: P < 0.001 (¶45)  
  - TAPSE/PASP ratio:  
    - Empagliflozin group: –0.17 (95% CI –0.25 to –0.09)  
    - Control group: –0.05 (95% CI –0.10 to –0.01)  
    - Between-group difference: P < 0.001 (¶45)  
  - Minnesota Living with Heart Failure Questionnaire (MLHFQ) score:  
    - Empagliflozin group: –4 (95% CI –6 to –2)  
    - Control group: +1 (95% CI –2 to 3)  
    - Between-group difference: P = 0.007 (¶39)  
  - Body weight:  
    - Empagliflozin group: –1.0 kg (95% CI –1.5 to 0.0)  
    - Control group: 0.0 kg (95% CI –0.5 to 0.5)  
    - Between-group difference: P = 0.027 (¶41)  
  - NT-proBNP and sST2 levels: Improved in the empagliflozin group compared with the control group (P < 0.05 for both) (¶3)  
- Follow-up time: 6 months (¶2)

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Randomized controlled open-label trial (¶2)  
- Randomization: 1:1 ratio (¶18)  
- Blinding: Open-label (¶2)  
- Number of centres: Single-center (¶2)  
- Registration: ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCT03753087 (¶5)  
- Follow-up duration: 6 months (¶2)

### Key findings relevant to the question
- Empagliflozin significantly improved the 6MWD compared to the control group (P = 0.033) (¶38: "After 6 months of therapy, the 6MWTD, the primary endpoint, increased by 16 (95% CI 8–24) m in the Empagliflozin group but only by 3 (95% CI–6–12) m in the control group")  
- Empagliflozin significantly reduced LA volume index, E/e′ ratio, and PASP compared to the control group (P < 0.001 for all) (¶42: "After 6 months, the empagliflozin group showed a significant decrease in key parameters associated with the LV filling pressure: the mean change in the mitral E/e′ ratio was − 1.4 (95% CI − 2.0 to − 1.0); in the LA volume index, it was − 2.9 (95% CI − 4.2 to − 1.6) mL/m 2 ; and in the PASP, it was − 4.2 (95% CI − 7.0 to − 1.5) mm Hg")  
- Empagliflozin improved LV diastolic function, LA reservoir and contractile function, and chronotropic reserves compared to the control group (P < 0.05 for all) (¶3: "LV diastolic, LA reservoir and contractile, and chronotropic reserves also improved in the empagliflozin group compared with those in the control group (P < 0.05 for all)")  
- Empagliflozin improved NT-proBNP and sST2 blood levels compared with those in the control group (P < 0.05 for both) (¶3: "Furthermore, treatment with empagliflozin led to improvements in NT-proBNP and ST2 blood levels compared with those in the control group (P < 0.05 for both)")  
- Empagliflozin improved the MLHFQ score compared to the control group (P = 0.007) (¶39: "An improvement in MLHFQ total score was revealed only in the empagliflozin group (a reduction by 4 [95% CI–6––2] units vs. an increase by 1 [95% CI–2–3] units in the control group")  
- Empagliflozin reduced body weight compared to the control group (P = 0.027) (¶41: "After 6 months, a decrease in body weight was observed in the empagliflozin group but not in the control group")  
- Empagliflozin improved TAPSE and TAPSE/PASP ratio compared to the control group (P < 0.001 for both) (¶45: "the empagliflozin group had an increase in TAPSE of 0.12 (95% CI 0.03–0.20) cm, whereas the control group had a decrease of 0.10 (95% CI − 0.17–0.03) cm, resulting in a significant between-group difference (P < 0.001")
