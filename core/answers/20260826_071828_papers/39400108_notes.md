### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- **Population**: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF)  
- **Inclusion criteria**: Patients with ejection fraction >40%  
- **Exclusion criteria**: Patients with reduced ejection fraction (<40%)  
- **Age/sex**: Adult patients (>18 years); no specific age or sex restrictions reported  
- **Setting**: Not explicitly stated  
- **Sample size**: 16,509 participants across 8 studies  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- **Drug/procedure**: SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin, sotogliflozin, ertugliflozin)  
- **Dose**:  
  - Empagliflozin: 10 mg or 25 mg per day  
  - Dapagliflozin: 10 mg per day  
  - Sotagliflozin: 200 mg uptitrated to 400 mg per day  
  - Ertugliflozin: 5 mg or 15 mg per day  
- **Frequency**: Daily  
- **Route**: Oral  
- **Duration**: Varies by study (12 weeks to 26.2 months)  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- **Comparator**: Placebo  
- **Dose & duration**: Placebo matched to the intervention in terms of frequency and duration  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- **Primary outcomes**:  
  - Cardiovascular death or hospitalization for HF:  
    - **Pooled results**: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (Mean Difference (MD) = 0.78; 95% CI = 0.72–0.85)  
    - **Heterogeneity**: I² = 0%, p = 0.63  
  - **Cardiovascular death**:  
    - **Pooled results**: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (MD = 0.92; 95% CI = 0.81–1.03)  
    - **Heterogeneity**: I² = 14%, p = 0.32  
  - **HF hospitalization**:  
    - **Pooled results**: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (MD = 0.74; 95% CI = 0.67–0.83)  
    - **Heterogeneity**: I² = 0%, p = 0.87  
  - **All-cause death**:  
    - **Pooled results**: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (MD = 0.97; 95% CI = 0.89–1.06)  
    - **Heterogeneity**: I² = 0%, p = 0.86  
- **Secondary outcomes**:  
  - **Any-cause death**:  
    - **Pooled results**: SGLT2i group vs. placebo group (MD = 0.97; 95% CI = 0.88–1.06)  
    - **Heterogeneity**: I² = 0%, p = 0.54  
- **Follow-up time**: Varies by study (12 weeks to 26.2 months)  
- **Adverse events**: Not explicitly detailed in the results section, but mentioned in the discussion as including genital fungal infections, urinary tract infections, hypotension, hypoglycemia, and ketoacidosis  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- **Study type**: Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials (RCTs)  
- **Blinding**: Not explicitly stated for all studies, but RCTs were included  
- **Randomization**: Yes, as RCTs were included  
- **Number of centres**: Not reported  
- **Registration**: Registered on PROSPERO with registration number CRD42023489104  
- **Follow-up duration**: Varies by study (12 weeks to 26.2 months)  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- SGLT2 inhibitors significantly reduced the risk of cardiovascular hospitalization in HFpEF patients (MD = 0.78; 95% CI = 0.72–0.85).  
- SGLT2 inhibitors significantly reduced the risk of HF hospitalization in HFpEF patients (MD = 0.74; 95% CI = 0.67–0.83).  
- SGLT2 inhibitors did not significantly reduce the risk of cardiovascular death (MD = 0.92; 95% CI = 0.81–1.03) or all-cause death (MD = 0.97; 95% CI = 0.89–1.06).  
- The benefits of SGLT2 inhibitors were consistent across subgroups, including baseline eGFR, systolic blood pressure, atrial fibrillation, diabetes, sex, BMI, and NT-proBNP.  
- The effect was heterogeneous in patients with NYHA class III or IV and in Black patients, with a trend toward less benefit in Black patients.  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The study included a moderate to high quality of RCTs, but heterogeneity was observed in some subgroups (e.g., NYHA class III or IV, Black race).  
- The study did not report long-term adverse events or effects on exercise tolerance.  
- The results for cardiovascular death and all-cause death were not statistically significant.  
- The study did not include all possible SGLT2 inhibitors or all relevant trials, as only those published in the last 5 years were included.
