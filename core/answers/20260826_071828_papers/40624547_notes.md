### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- **Population**: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) and type 2 diabetes (T2D).  
- **Inclusion criteria**: Adults aged 40–80 years with LVEF ≥ 50%, evidence of elevated left ventricular filling pressures, T2D with HbA1c 7.0–10.0% at baseline, and myocardial fibrosis (ECV ≥ 27% on cardiac MRI).  
- **Exclusion criteria**: Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m²), active malignancy within the past 5 years, contraindications to MRI, and hypersensitivity to dapagliflozin.  
- **Setting**: Multicenter trial at three tertiary care centers specializing in HFpEF and diabetes management.  
- **Sample size**: 100 patients (50 per arm).  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- **Intervention**: Dapagliflozin 10 mg daily.  
- **Dose**: 10 mg daily.  
- **Frequency**: Daily.  
- **Route**: Oral.  
- **Duration**: 12 months.  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- **Comparator**: Placebo.  
- **Dose & Duration**: Placebo administered daily for 12 months.  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- **Primary outcome**: Change in myocardial fibrosis, measured by ECV (%) from baseline to 12 months.  
  - Dapagliflozin: −3.5% (95% CI −4.2 to −2.8).  
  - Placebo: −0.8% (95% CI −1.3 to −0.4).  
  - p < 0.001.  
- **Secondary outcomes**:  
  - Change in left ventricular mass index (LVMI):  
    - Dapagliflozin: −8.2 g/m² (95% CI −9.5 to −7.0).  
    - Placebo: −2.1 g/m² (95% CI −3.0 to −1.2).  
    - p = 0.002.  
  - Change in HbA1c:  
    - Dapagliflozin: −1.2% (95% CI −1.5 to −1.0).  
    - Placebo: −0.4% (95% CI −0.6 to −0.2).  
    - p = 0.01.  
  - Change in 6-minute walk test (6MWT) distance:  
    - Dapagliflozin: +45 m (95% CI 35–55).  
    - Placebo: +10 m (95% CI 5–15).  
    - p = 0.01.  
  - Change in NT-proBNP:  
    - Dapagliflozin: −210 pg/mL (95% CI −250 to −170).  
    - Placebo: −50 pg/mL (95% CI −120 to +20).  
    - p = 0.008.  
- **Follow-up time**: 12 months.  
- **Adverse events**: Minor adverse effects included mild volume depletion and urinary tract symptoms in the dapagliflozin group. No serious adverse events related to study medication.  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- **Study design**: Multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized trial.  
- **Blinding**: Both patients and investigators were blinded to treatment allocation.  
- **Randomization**: Computer-generated sequence, stratified by baseline ECV (<30% vs. ≥30%).  
- **Number of centres**: Three tertiary care centers.  
- **Registration**: Not explicitly mentioned.  
- **Follow-up duration**: 12 months.  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Dapagliflozin significantly reduced myocardial fibrosis (mean ΔECV: −3.5% vs. −0.8% in placebo; p < 0.001).  
- Dapagliflozin improved LVMI (−8.2 g/m² vs. −2.1 g/m²; p = 0.002).  
- Dapagliflozin improved glycemic control (HbA1c reduction: −1.2% vs. −0.4%; p = 0.01).  
- Dapagliflozin enhanced functional capacity (6MWT: +45 m vs. +10 m; p = 0.01).  
- Dapagliflozin reduced NT-proBNP levels (−210 pg/mL vs. −50 pg/mL; p = 0.008).  
- Dapagliflozin showed a trend toward improved hospitalization-free survival (92% vs. 80% at 12 months; p = 0.06).  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The modest sample size and short follow-up duration may limit generalizability and statistical power for rare events.  
- No tissue-based or molecular markers (e.g., TGF-β, collagen turnover) were included to confirm antifibrotic mechanisms.  
- The cohort consisted largely of Middle Eastern patients, which may limit extrapolation to other populations.  
- LGE burden or patterns were not assessed, which could provide complementary insights into fibrosis characterization.
