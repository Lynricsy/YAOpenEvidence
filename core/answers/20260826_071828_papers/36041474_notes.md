### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with heart failure (HF) with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF and HFmrEF)  
- Inclusion criteria: Patients with heart failure, including those admitted to hospital with worsening heart failure (irrespective of ejection fraction)  
- Sample size: 12,251 participants from DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved; 21,947 participants across all five trials

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Time from randomisation to the composite of cardiovascular death or first hospitalisation for heart failure  
  - Hazard ratio (HR): 0.80 [95% CI 0.73–0.87] in DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved (12,251 participants)  
  - HR across all five trials (21,947 participants): 0.77 [0.72–0.82]  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - Cardiovascular death: HR 0.88 [0.77–1.00] in DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved; HR 0.87 [0.79–0.95] across all five trials  
  - First hospitalisation for heart failure: HR 0.74 [0.67–0.83] in DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved; HR 0.72 [0.67–0.78] across all five trials  
  - All-cause mortality: HR 0.92 [0.86–0.99] across all five trials  
- Follow-up time: Not explicitly reported  
- Adverse events: Not reported  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Meta-analysis of five randomised controlled trials (RCTs)  
- Blinding: Not reported  
- Randomization: Yes, as per the included RCTs  
- Number of centres: Not reported  
- Registration: Registered with PROSPERO, CRD42022327527  
- Follow-up duration: Not explicitly reported  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of cardiovascular death or first hospitalisation for heart failure in patients with HFpEF and HFmrEF (HR 0.80 [0.73–0.87] in DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved).  
- The benefit was consistent across all five trials (HR 0.77 [0.72–0.82] for the composite endpoint).  
- Reductions were observed in both cardiovascular death (HR 0.87 [0.79–0.95]) and first hospitalisation for heart failure (HR 0.72 [0.67–0.78]).  
- Treatment effects were consistent across subgroups, including ejection fraction.
