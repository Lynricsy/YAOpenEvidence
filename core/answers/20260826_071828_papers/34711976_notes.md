### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- **Population**: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF).  
- **Inclusion criteria**: Adults with clinical diagnosis of HFpEF, LVEF ≥ 45%, NYHA class II–IV symptoms, elevated natriuretic peptides (NTproBNP ≥ 225 or BNP ≥ 75 pg/ml; if AF, NTproBNP ≥ 375 or BNP ≥ 100 pg/ml), requirement for diuretic therapy, and either HF hospitalization or urgent HF visit with intravenous diuretic treatment in the past 12 months.  
- **Exclusion criteria**: Recent hospitalization (within 7 days) for decompensated HF, eGFR < 20 ml/min/1.73 m², type 1 diabetes, or previous history of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA).  
- **Age/sex**: Median age 70.0 years (IQR 63.0–77.0), 57% women.  
- **Setting**: Multicenter trial in the United States (26 sites).  
- **Sample size**: 324 patients randomized (162 to dapagliflozin, 162 to placebo).  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- **Intervention**: Dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily.  
- **Dose**: 10 mg.  
- **Frequency**: Once daily.  
- **Route**: Oral.  
- **Duration**: 12 weeks.  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- **Comparator**: Placebo.  
- **Dose & duration**: Placebo once daily for 12 weeks.  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- **Primary outcome**: Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Clinical Summary Score (KCCQ-CS) at 12 weeks.  
  - **Result**: Dapagliflozin improved KCCQ-CS by 5.8 points (95% CI 2.3–9.2, P = 0.001).  
- **Secondary outcomes**:  
  - **6-minute walk test (6MWT)**: Mean improvement of 20.1 m (95% CI 5.6–34.7, P = 0.007).  
  - **KCCQ Overall Summary Score (KCCQ-OS)**: 4.5 points (95% CI 1.1–7.8, P = 0.009).  
  - **Proportion of participants with ≥5-point improvement in KCCQ-OS**: Odds ratio (OR) = 1.73 (95% CI 1.05–2.85, P = 0.03).  
  - **Weight reduction**: Mean effect size of 0.72 kg (95% CI 0.01–1.42, P = 0.046).  
- **Follow-up time**: 12 weeks.  
- **Adverse events**: Similar between groups (44 (27.2%) vs. 38 (23.5%) patients).  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- **Study design**: Multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (NCT03030235).  
- **Blinding**: Double-blind.  
- **Randomization**: 1:1 randomization to dapagliflozin or placebo.  
- **Number of centres**: 26 sites in the United States.  
- **Registration**: NCT03030235.  
- **Follow-up duration**: 12 weeks.  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Dapagliflozin significantly improved patient-reported symptoms (KCCQ-CS) by 5.8 points (95% CI 2.3–9.2, P = 0.001) at 12 weeks.  
- Dapagliflozin improved physical limitations (KCCQ-PL) by 5.3 points (95% CI 0.7–10.0, P = 0.026).  
- Dapagliflozin improved 6MWT by 20.1 m (95% CI 5.6–34.7, P = 0.007).  
- Dapagliflozin improved KCCQ-OS by 4.5 points (95% CI 1.1–7.8, P = 0.009).  
- Dapagliflozin was associated with a 0.72 kg (95% CI 0.01–1.42, P = 0.046) reduction in weight.  
- The benefits were consistent across subgroups, including patients with and without type 2 diabetes (T2D) and those with ejection fraction above and below 60%.  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- **Short follow-up duration**: The 12-week follow-up precludes assessment of the durability of the observed benefits.  
- **Geographic limitation**: All patients were enrolled in the United States, which may limit generalizability to other populations.  
- **Lack of long-term data**: The study does not provide information on long-term outcomes or the sustainability of the observed improvements.
