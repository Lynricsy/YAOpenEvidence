### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF)  
- Inclusion criteria: Class II-IV heart failure with ejection fraction >40%  
- Exclusion criteria: Not explicitly stated  
- Age/sex: Not explicitly stated  
- Setting: Double-blind trial  
- Sample size: 5988 patients (2997 in empagliflozin group, 2991 in placebo group)

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Drug: Empagliflozin  
- Dose: 10 mg once daily  
- Route: Oral  
- Duration: Not explicitly stated, but follow-up was a median of 26.2 months  
- Additional therapy: Usual therapy (not specified)

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo  
- Dose & duration: Placebo administered once daily for the same duration as empagliflozin

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure  
  - Empagliflozin group: 415 of 2997 patients (13.8%)  
  - Placebo group: 511 of 2991 patients (17.1%)  
  - Hazard ratio: 0.79 (95% CI, 0.69 to 0.90; P<0.001)  
- Secondary outcome: Hospitalization for heart failure  
  - Empagliflozin group: 407 hospitalizations  
  - Placebo group: 541 hospitalizations  
  - Hazard ratio: 0.73 (95% CI, 0.61 to 0.88; P<0.001)  
- Adverse events:  
  - Uncomplicated genital and urinary tract infections  
  - Hypotension  
  - More frequently reported with empagliflozin  
- Follow-up time: Median of 26.2 months

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Double-blind, randomized controlled trial (RCT)  
- Blinding: Double-blind  
- Randomization: Yes  
- Number of centres: Not explicitly stated  
- Registration: Yes (ClinicalTrials.gov number, NCT03057951)  
- Follow-up duration: Median of 26.2 months

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Empagliflozin reduced the combined risk of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure in patients with HFpEF (HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.69–0.90, P<0.001).  
- The risk reduction was mainly due to a lower risk of hospitalization for heart failure (HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.61–0.88, P<0.001).  
- The effect was consistent in patients with or without diabetes.  
- More frequent reports of genital and urinary tract infections and hypotension were observed in the empagliflozin group.
