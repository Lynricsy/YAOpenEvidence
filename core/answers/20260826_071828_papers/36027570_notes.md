### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF)  
- Inclusion criteria: Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) > 40%  
- Exclusion criteria: Not explicitly stated  
- Age/sex: Not explicitly stated  
- Setting: Not explicitly stated  
- Sample size: 6263 patients (3131 in dapagliflozin group, 3132 in placebo group)

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Dapagliflozin (10 mg once daily)  
- Dose: 10 mg once daily  
- Frequency: Once daily  
- Route: Oral  
- Duration: Median of 2.3 years

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Matching placebo  
- Dose & duration: Same as intervention (placebo administered once daily for 2.3 years)

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Composite of worsening heart failure (unplanned hospitalization for heart failure or urgent visit for heart failure) or cardiovascular death  
  - Dapagliflozin group: 512 of 3131 patients (16.4%)  
  - Placebo group: 610 of 3132 patients (19.5%)  
  - Hazard ratio (HR): 0.82 (95% CI, 0.73 to 0.92; P < 0.001)  
- Worsening heart failure:  
  - Dapagliflozin group: 368 of 3131 patients (11.8%)  
  - Placebo group: 455 of 3132 patients (14.5%)  
  - HR: 0.79 (95% CI, 0.69 to 0.91)  
- Cardiovascular death:  
  - Dapagliflozin group: 231 of 3131 patients (7.4%)  
  - Placebo group: 261 of 3132 patients (8.3%)  
  - HR: 0.88 (95% CI, 0.74 to 1.05)  
- Follow-up time: Median of 2.3 years  
- Adverse events: Similar incidence in both groups

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Randomized controlled trial (RCT)  
- Blinding: Double-blind (patients and investigators)  
- Randomization: Random assignment  
- Number of centres: Not explicitly stated  
- Registration: Yes (ClinicalTrials.gov number, NCT03619213)  
- Follow-up duration: Median of 2.3 years

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Dapagliflozin reduced the combined risk of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death in patients with HFpEF (HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.73–0.92, P < 0.001).  
- Worsening heart failure was less frequent in the dapagliflozin group (HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.69–0.91).  
- Cardiovascular death rates were similar between groups (HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.74–1.05).  
- Benefits were consistent across subgroups, including those with LVEF ≥ 60% and < 60%, and with or without diabetes.  
- Adverse event rates were similar between the dapagliflozin and placebo groups.
