### Relevance (3)
### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- **Population**: Patients with chronic heart failure (HF), including both heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF) and heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF).  
- **Inclusion criteria**: Patients with or without type 2 diabetes (T2DM) who received empagliflozin (EMPA) in RCTs.  
- **Exclusion criteria**: Acute HF patients were excluded.  
- **Age**: Age ranges varied across studies, with a mean age of 64.2–75.0 years.  
- **Sex**: Female sex percentages ranged from 13.0% to 44.7% across studies.  
- **Setting**: Multinational clinical trials.  
- **Sample size**: Total of 5503 patients in the EMPA group and 5486 patients in the control group across nine RCTs.  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- **Intervention**: Empagliflozin (EMPA), an SGLT2 inhibitor.  
- **Dose**: 10 mg once daily.  
- **Frequency**: Once daily.  
- **Route**: Oral.  
- **Duration**: Varies across studies, with a minimum of 12 weeks and a maximum of 26.2 months.  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- **Comparator**: Placebo or standard care.  
- **Dose & Duration**: Placebo or standard care, with duration matching the EMPA treatment period.  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- **Primary outcomes**:  
  - **Cardiac remodeling**:  
    - Left ventricular end-diastolic volume (LVEDV): MD = -18.05 mL (95% CI: -25.3 to -10.80), P < 0.0001.  
    - Left ventricular end-systolic volume (LVESV): MD = -16.05 mL (95% CI: -27.59 to -4.50), P = 0.006.  
    - N-terminal pro-B-type NP (NT-proBNP): SMD = -0.50 (95% CI: -0.74 to -0.26), P < 0.0001.  
  - **Exercise capacity**:  
    - 6-minute walk distance (6MWD): MD = 27.50 m (95% CI: 9.02 to 45.97), P = 0.004.  
  - **Quality of life (QoL)**:  
    - Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire (KCCQ) score: MD = 2.72 (95% CI: 1.27 to 4.12), P < 0.0001.  
- **Secondary outcomes**:  
  - **Blood pressure**:  
    - Systolic blood pressure (SBP): MD = -1.19 mmHg (95% CI: -8.56 to -0.66), P = 0.001.  
  - **Adverse events (AEs)**:  
    - Increased risk of urinary tract infections (log OR = 0.34, P < 0.0001).  
    - Increased risk of cardiovascular AEs in HFpEF patients (P < 0.0001).  
- **Follow-up time**: Varies across studies, with a maximum of 26.2 months.  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- **Study design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs.  
- **Blinding**: Studies were double-blind where applicable.  
- **Randomization**: Randomized controlled trials (RCTs).  
- **Number of centres**: Multinational trials.  
- **Registration**: The study protocol was registered in PROSPERO (CRD42024570205).  
- **Follow-up duration**: Varies across studies, with a maximum of 26.2 months.  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- **HFpEF patients**:  
  - EMPA significantly reduced NT-proBNP levels (SMD = -0.85, 95% CI: -1.73 to 0.03, P = 0.058).  
  - No significant improvement in 6MWD (MD = 20.83 m, 95% CI: -10.62 to 52.29, P = 0.194).  
  - Limited improvement in KCCQ scores (MD = 1.33, 95% CI: 1.31 to 1.35, P < 0.0001).  
  - Increased risk of cardiovascular AEs (P < 0.0001).  
- **HFrEF patients**:  
  - Greater improvements in 6MWD (MD = 34.33 m, P < 0.0001) and KCCQ scores (MD = 5.00, 95% CI: 0.92 to 9.07, P = 0.016).  
  - Significant reduction in LVEDV and LVESV.  
- **Long-term treatment (≥6 months)**:  
  - Greater reduction in LVEDV (MD = -21.31 mL, 95% CI: -27.67 to -14.96, P < 0.0001) and LVESV (MD = -21.95 mL, 95% CI: -27.32 to -16.59, P < 0.0001).  
  - Improved KCCQ scores (MD = 1.33, 95% CI: 1.31 to 1.35, P < 0.0001).  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- **Limitations**:  
  - Data on left ventricular remodeling in HFpEF patients treated with EMPA are limited.  
  - The study did not provide detailed data on the long-term safety profile of EMPA in HFpEF patients.  
  - The heterogeneity in study designs and outcomes may affect the generalizability of the findings.  
  - The study did not include all relevant RCTs due to the exclusion of short-term trials.
