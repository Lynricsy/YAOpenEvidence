### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF: ≥50%) or mildly reduced ejection fraction (HFmrEF: 41-49%)  
- Inclusion criteria: Patients receiving SGLT2 inhibitors (SGLT2i) vs. placebo; regardless of baseline diabetes  
- Sample size: 15,769 patients (HFmrEF/HFpEF combined)  
- Subgroup sizes:  
  - HFpEF: N = 8,891  
  - HFmrEF: N = 4,555  
  - HFmrEF/HFpEF without baseline diabetes: N = 6,507  
- Setting: Not explicitly reported  
- Age/sex: Not explicitly reported

- Intervention: Sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 inhibitors (SGLT2i)  
- Comparator: Placebo  
- Dose, frequency, route, duration: Not explicitly reported

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Cardiovascular death (CVD) and/or urgent visits/hospitalization for heart failure (HHF)  
- Pooled HR for CVD/HHF outcomes in HFmrEF/HFpEF: 0.80 (95% CI: 0.74, 0.86, p < 0.001)  
- HR for HFpEF subgroup: 0.79 (95% CI: 0.71, 0.87, p < 0.001)  
- HR for HFmrEF subgroup: 0.77 (95% CI: 0.67, 0.89, p < 0.001)  
- HR for HFmrEF/HFpEF without baseline diabetes: 0.80 (95% CI: 0.70, 0.91, p < 0.001)  
- Sensitivity analysis (including DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved trials) for CV deaths: HR 0.90 (95% CI: 0.79, 1.02, p = 0.08)

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- SGLT2i use was significantly associated with improved cardiovascular death (CVD) and/or heart failure hospitalization (HHF) outcomes in patients with HFpEF and HFmrEF.  
- Pooled HR for CVD/HHF outcomes in HFmrEF/HFpEF: 0.80 (95% CI: 0.74, 0.86, p < 0.001).  
- Benefits were consistent in HFpEF (HR 0.79, 95% CI: 0.71, 0.87, p < 0.001) and HFmrEF (HR 0.77, 95% CI: 0.67, 0.89, p < 0.001).  
- Benefits were also observed in patients without baseline diabetes (HR 0.80, 95% CI: 0.70, 0.91, p < 0.001).  
- Sensitivity analysis showed a trend toward significant beneficial effects on CV deaths (HR 0.90, 95% CI: 0.79, 1.02, p = 0.08).
