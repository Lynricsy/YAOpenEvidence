### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Dapagliflozin was one of the SGLT2 inhibitors assessed in the meta-analysis (¶3: "assessing three SGLT2 inhibitors: empagliflozin (EMPA-REG OUTCOME), canagliflozin (CANVAS Program and CREDENCE), and dapagliflozin (DECLARE-TIMI 58)")  
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of dialysis, transplantation, or death due to kidney disease (RR 0.67, 95% CI 0.52-0.86, p=0.0019) (¶3: "SGLT2 inhibitors substantially reduced the risk of dialysis, transplantation, or death due to kidney disease (RR 0·67, 95% CI 0·52-0·86, p=0·0019)")  
- The effect was consistent across studies (I²=0%, p heterogeneity=0.53) (¶3: "an effect consistent across studies (I2=0%, pheterogeneity=0·53)")  
- SGLT2 inhibitors also reduced end-stage kidney disease (RR 0.65, 95% CI 0.53-0.81, p<0.0001) (¶3: "SGLT2 inhibitors also reduced end-stage kidney disease (0·65, 0·53-0·81, p<0·0001)")  
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduced acute kidney injury (RR 0.75, 95% CI 0.66-0.85, p<0.0001) (¶3: "and acute kidney injury (0·75, 0·66-0·85, p<0·0001)")  
- The benefit was consistent across eGFR subgroups, including for participants with a baseline eGFR 30-45 mL/min per 1.73 m² (RR 0.70, 95% CI 0.54-0.91, p=0.0080) (¶3: "clear, separate evidence of benefit for all eGFR subgroups, including for participants with a baseline eGFR 30-45 mL/min per 1·73 m2 (RR 0·70, 95% CI 0·54-0·91, p=0·0080)")  
- Renoprotection was consistent across studies irrespective of baseline albuminuria (p trend=0.66) and use of RAS blockade (p heterogeneity=0.31) (¶4: "Renoprotection was also consistent across studies irrespective of baseline albuminuria (ptrend=0·66) and use of RAS blockade (pheterogeneity=0·31)")
