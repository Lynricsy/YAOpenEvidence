- Patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus

### I — Intervention / 干预措施

- Sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 inhibitors (SGLT2i)  
- Not reported: Dose, frequency, route, duration  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Not explicitly stated; the study compares the relative benefits of GLP1-RA and SGLT2i

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes: Composite of myocardial infarction, stroke, and cardiovascular death (MACE); hospitalization for heart failure; progression of kidney disease  
- GLP1-RA reduced MACE by 12% (HR, 0.88; 95% CI, 0.84-0.94; P<0.001)  
- SGLT2i reduced MACE by 11% (HR, 0.89; 95% CI, 0.83-0.96; P=0.001)  
- SGLT2i reduced hospitalization for heart failure by 31% (HR, 0.69; 95% CI, 0.61-0.79; P<0.001)  
- GLP1-RA did not significantly reduce hospitalization for heart failure (HR, 0.93; 95% CI, 0.83-1.04; P=0.20)  
- Both GLP1-RA and SGLT2i reduced the risk of progression of kidney disease (GLP1-RA HR, 0.82; 95% CI, 0.75-0.89; P<0.001; SGLT2i HR, 0.62; 95% CI, 0.58-0.67; P<0.001)  
- SGLT2i reduced the risk of worsening estimated glomerular filtration rate, end-stage kidney disease, or renal death (HR, 0.55; 95% CI, 0.48-0.64; P<0.001)  
- Follow-up time: Not explicitly stated  
- Adverse events: Not reported
