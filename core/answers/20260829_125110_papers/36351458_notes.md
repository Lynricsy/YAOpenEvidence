### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with type 2 diabetes and high risk of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, heart failure, or chronic kidney disease (CKD).  
- Inclusion criteria: Adults (≥18 years), large trials (≥500 participants per group), at least 6 months in duration.  
- Exclusion criteria: Not explicitly stated, but trials with follow-up <6 months were excluded.  
- Age/sex: Trial-level mean age ranged from 61.9 to 71.8 years; 35.7% of patients were women.  
- Setting: Multicenter trials.  
- Sample size: 90,413 participants in total (74,804 [82.7%] with diabetes, 15,605 [17.3%] without diabetes).  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Sodium glucose co-transporter-2 (SGLT2) inhibitors (e.g., dapagliflozin, canagliflozin, empagliflozin, ertugliflozin, sotagliflozin).  
- Dose: Varies by trial (e.g., dapagliflozin 10 mg, canagliflozin 100–300 mg, empagliflozin 10 mg or 25 mg, ertugliflozin 5 mg or 15 mg, sotagliflozin 200–400 mg).  
- Frequency: Daily.  
- Route: Oral.  
- Duration: At least 6 months, with median follow-up ranging from 0.8 to 4.2 years.  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo.  
- Dose & duration: Placebo was administered for the same duration as the SGLT2 inhibitor.  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- **Primary outcomes**:  
  - Kidney disease progression (defined as a sustained ≥50% decrease in eGFR from randomisation, sustained low eGFR, end-stage kidney disease, or death from kidney failure).  
    - RR: 0.63 (95% CI 0.58–0.69) for SGLT2 inhibitors vs. placebo.  
    - Similar in patients with and without diabetes.  
  - Acute kidney injury: RR 0.77 (0.70–0.84).  
  - Composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure: RR 0.77 (0.74–0.81).  
- **Secondary outcomes**:  
  - Cardiovascular death: RR 0.86 (0.81–0.92).  
  - Non-cardiovascular death: RR 0.94 (0.88–1.02).  
  - Safety outcomes:  
    - Ketoacidosis: Not explicitly reported as a significant outcome.  
    - Lower limb amputation: Not explicitly reported as a significant outcome.  
- Follow-up time: Median follow-up ranged from 0.8 to 4.2 years.  
- Adverse events: Not explicitly reported in detail, but ketoacidosis and lower limb amputation were considered safety outcomes.  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs.  
- Blinding: Double-blind.  
- Randomization: Yes, as all included trials were RCTs.  
- Number of centres: Not explicitly stated, but trials were multicenter.  
- Registration: Registered with PROSPERO, CRD42022351618.  
- Follow-up duration: Median follow-up ranged from 0.8 to 4.2 years.  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of kidney disease progression by 37% (RR 0.63, 95% CI 0.58–0.69) in patients with type 2 diabetes (¶3: "Compared with placebo, allocation to an SGLT2 inhibitor reduced the risk of kidney disease progression by 37% (relative risk [RR] 0·63, 95% CI 0·58–0·69) with similar RRs in patients with and without diabetes").  
- The effect on kidney disease progression was similar in patients with and without diabetes (¶3: "with similar RRs in patients with and without diabetes").  
- In chronic kidney disease trials, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of kidney disease progression by 40% (RR 0.60, 95% CI 0.53–0.69) in patients with diabetic kidney disease (¶31: "SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of kidney disease progression by 40% (0·60, 0·53–0·69)").  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Limited data on patients without diabetes in previous meta-analyses (¶9: "data were limited in patients without diabetes who were eligible for inclusion").  
- Inability to standardise between-trial differences in the thresholds of eGFR decrease used to define kidney disease progression (¶13: "the inability to standardise between-trial differences in the thresholds of eGFR decrease used to define kidney disease progression within categorical composite outcomes").  
- Lack of individual participant-level data (¶21: "absence of individual participant-level data").  
- Exclusion of trials with follow-up <6 months (¶25: "Two large trials [...] were excluded from meta-analyses as follow-up was less than 6 months").
