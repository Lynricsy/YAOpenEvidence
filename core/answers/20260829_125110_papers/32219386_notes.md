### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF) with and without diabetes  
- Inclusion criteria: New York Heart Association classification II to IV, ejection fraction ≤ 40%, elevated plasma N-terminal pro B-type natriuretic peptide  
- Exclusion criteria: Not reported  
- Age: Mean age 66 years  
- Sex: 1109 (23%) women  
- Setting: 410 sites in 20 countries  
- Sample size: 4744 patients randomized (2605 [55%] without diabetes)  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Addition of once-daily 10 mg of dapagliflozin to recommended therapy  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo added to recommended therapy  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Composite of an episode of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death  
  - In patients without diabetes: 13.2% in dapagliflozin group vs 17.7% in placebo group (HR 0.73 [95% CI, 0.60-0.88])  
  - In patients with diabetes: 20.0% in dapagliflozin group vs 25.5% in placebo group (HR 0.75 [95% CI, 0.63-0.90])  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - Volume depletion: 7.3% in dapagliflozin group vs 6.1% in placebo group among patients without diabetes; 7.8% in dapagliflozin group vs 7.8% in placebo group among patients with diabetes  
  - Kidney adverse events: 4.8% in dapagliflozin group vs 6.0% in placebo group among patients without diabetes; 8.5% in dapagliflozin group vs 8.7% in placebo group among patients with diabetes  
- Follow-up time: Final follow-up on June 6, 2019  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Phase 3 randomized trial  
- Blinding: Not explicitly stated  
- Randomization: Yes  
- Number of centres: 410 sites in 20 countries  
- Registration: ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCT03036124  
- Follow-up duration: Not explicitly stated  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Dapagliflozin significantly reduced the risk of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death in patients with HFrEF, independently of diabetes status (¶8: "In this exploratory analysis of a randomized trial of patients with HFrEF, dapagliflozin compared with placebo, when added to recommended therapy, significantly reduced the risk of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death independently of diabetes status.")  
- In patients without diabetes, the primary outcome occurred in 171 of 1298 (13.2%) in the dapagliflozin group and 231 of 1307 (17.7%) in the placebo group (HR 0.73 [95% CI, 0.60-0.88]) (¶6: "Among participants without diabetes, the primary outcome occurred in 171 of 1298 (13.2%) in the dapagliflozin group and 231 of 1307 (17.7%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.73 [95% CI, 0.60-0.88]).")  
- In patients with diabetes, the primary outcome occurred in 215 of 1075 (20.0%) in the dapagliflozin group and 271 of 1064 (25.5%) in the placebo group (HR 0.75 [95% CI, 0.63-0.90]) (¶6: "In patients with diabetes, the primary outcome occurred in 215 of 1075 (20.0%) in the dapagliflozin group and 271 of 1064 (25.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.75 [95% CI, 0.63-0.90]).")
