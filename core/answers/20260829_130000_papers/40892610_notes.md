### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- The study included 99,599 patients from 21 randomized controlled trials.  
- Patients were from diverse populations, including those with diabetes mellitus, kidney function issues, obesity, or heart failure.  
- Specific subgroups were analyzed based on diabetes mellitus, kidney function, obesity, or heart failure.  
- The study did not specify inclusion or exclusion criteria, age, sex, or exact setting.  
- Sample size per arm was not reported.  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RAs) were used, including lixisenatide, liraglutide, exenatide, semaglutide, efpeglenatide, dulaglutide, albiglutide, and tirzepatide.  
- Each GLP-1 RA was administered at therapeutic doses.  
- The duration of administration was not explicitly stated, but the mean follow-up duration was 2.4 years.  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- GLP-1 RAs were compared with controls or placebo.  
- The specific doses or duration of the comparator was not reported.  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- **Primary outcomes**:  
  - All-cause death: IRR 0.88 (95% CI: 0.84-0.92; NNT = 121).  
  - CV death: IRR 0.87 (95% CI: 0.81-0.92; NNT = 170).  
  - MACE: IRR 0.87 (95% CI: 0.83-0.91; NNT = 66).  
- **Secondary outcomes**:  
  - Serious adverse events: -9%.  
  - Myocardial infarction: -15%.  
  - Acute kidney failure: -9%.  
  - Heart failure: -15%.  
  - Infections: -10%.  
  - Gastrointestinal disorders: +63%.  
  - Gallbladder disorders: +26%.  
- No differences were found in stroke, pancreatitis, or neoplasm.  
- Follow-up duration was 2.4 years.  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- The study was a systematic review and meta-analysis of 21 randomized controlled trials.  
- Subgroup analyses were conducted based on GLP-1 RA type, diabetes mellitus, kidney function, obesity, or heart failure.  
- GRADE and trial sequential analyses were used to evaluate certainty and conclusiveness of findings.  
- The study was registered with PROSPERO (CRD420251032222).  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- GLP-1 RAs reduced all-cause death, CV death, and MACE compared with controls (¶4: "We found conclusive, high-certainty evidence that GLP-1 RAs reduced all-cause death (incidence rate ratio [IRR]: 0.88; 95% CI: 0.84-0.92; needed to treat [NNT] = 121), CV death (IRR: 0.87; 95% CI: 0.81-0.92; NNT = 170), and MACE (IRR: 0.87; 95% CI: 0.83-0.91; NNT = 66), compared with controls.")  
- GLP-1 RAs reduced serious adverse events, myocardial infarction, acute kidney failure, heart failure, and infections (¶4: "GLP-1 RAs reduced serious adverse events (-9%), myocardial infarction (-15%), acute kidney failure (-9%), heart failure (-15%), and infections (-10%), but increased gastrointestinal (+63%) and gallbladder (+26%) disorders.")  
- There were no differences in stroke, pancreatitis, or neoplasm between groups (¶4: "There were no differences in stroke, pancreatitis, or neoplasm between groups.")  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The study did not report limitations explicitly.  
- However, the abstract mentions that previous meta-analyses were limited by restricted populations, omission of recent trials, or incomplete safety synthesis (¶2: "Previous meta-analyses of GLP-1 RAs have been limited by restricted populations, omission of recent trials, or incomplete safety synthesis; this study integrates the latest evidence across 21 randomized controlled trials and diverse populations using advanced meta-analytic methods.")
