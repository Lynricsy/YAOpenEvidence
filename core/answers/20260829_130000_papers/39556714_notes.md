### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) and obesity (body mass index ≥30 kg/m²) (¶10: "Patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction and obesity have significant disability and frequent exacerbations of heart failure.")  
- Inclusion criteria: Age ≥40 years, chronic New York Heart Association (NYHA) class II to IV HF, left ventricular ejection fraction ≥50%, BMI ≥30 kg/m², substantial symptoms and functional limitation attributable to HFpEF (as reflected by a 6-minute walk distance of 100 to 425 m and a Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Clinical Summary Score ≤80 at baseline), and objective evidence of HF (elevated NT-proBNP, structural heart disease, or elevated filling pressures). Patients were also required to have either HF decompensation in the preceding 12 months or an estimated glomerular filtration rate <70 mL·min⁻¹·1.73 m⁻² (¶10: "Participants with HFpEF who were ≥40 years of age with chronic New York Heart Association (NYHA) class II to IV HF, left ventricular ejection fraction ≥50%, and BMI ≥30 kg/m 2 were enrolled.")  
- Exclusion criteria: Not explicitly stated in the text.  
- Age: 65.2±10.7 years (¶3: "Patients were 65.2±10.7 years of age; 53.8% (n=393) were female; body mass index was 38.2±6.7 kg/m 2 ; Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Clinical Summary Score was 53.5±18.5; 6-minute walk distance was 302.8±81.7 m; and 53% (n=388) had a worsening heart failure event in the previous 12 months.")  
- Sex: 53.8% female (¶3: "Patients were 65.2±10.7 years of age; 53.8% (n=393) were female; body mass index was 38.2±6.7 kg/m 2 ; Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Clinical Summary Score was 53.5±18.5; 6-minute walk distance was 302.8±81.7 m; and 53% (n=388) had a worsening heart failure event in the previous 12 months.")  
- Setting: 129 centers in 9 countries (¶22: "A total of 731 patients with HFpEF and obesity were randomly assigned to receive tirzepatide (n=364) or placebo (n=367) at 129 centers in 9 countries (Figure S1 ).")  
- Sample size: 731 patients (n=364 tirzepatide, n=367 placebo) (¶22: "A total of 731 patients with HFpEF and obesity were randomly assigned to receive tirzepatide (n=364) or placebo (n=367) at 129 centers in 9 countries (Figure S1 ).")

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Tirzepatide, a long-acting agonist of glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide and glucagon-like peptide-1 receptors (¶1: "We hypothesized that tirzepatide, a long-acting agonist of glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide and glucagon-like peptide-1 receptors, would improve a comprehensive suite of clinical end points, including measures of health status, functional capacity, quality of life, exercise tolerance, patient well-being, and medication burden, in these patients.")  
- Dose: Titration up to 15 mg SC weekly (¶2: "We randomized (double-blind) 731 patients with class II to IV heart failure, ejection fraction ≥50%, and body mass index ≥30 kg/m 2 to tirzepatide (titrated up to 15 mg SC weekly; n=364) or placebo (n=367) added to background therapy for a median of 104 weeks (quartile 1, 66; quartile 3, 126 weeks).")  
- Frequency: Weekly (¶2: "We randomized (double-blind) 731 patients with class II to IV heart failure, ejection fraction ≥50%, and body mass index ≥30 kg/m 2 to tirzepatide (titrated up to 15 mg SC weekly; n=364) or placebo (n=367) added to background therapy for a median of 104 weeks (quartile 1, 66; quartile 3, 126 weeks).")  
- Route: Subcutaneous (SC) (¶2: "We randomized (double-blind) 731 patients with class II to IV heart failure, ejection fraction ≥50%, and body mass index ≥30 kg/m 2 to tirzepatide (titrated up to 15 mg SC weekly; n=364) or placebo (n=367) added to background therapy for a median of 104 weeks (quartile 1, 66; quartile 3, 126 weeks).")  
- Duration: Median of 104 weeks (quartile 1, 66; quartile 3, 126 weeks) (¶2: "We randomized (double-blind) 731 patients with class II to IV heart failure, ejection fraction ≥50%, and body mass index ≥30 kg/m 2 to tirzepatide (titrated up to 15 mg SC weekly; n=364) or placebo (n=367) added to background therapy for a median of 104 weeks (quartile 1, 66; quartile 3, 126 weeks).")

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes:  
  - Time to first worsening HF event or cardiovascular death (hazard ratio, 0.62 [95% CI, 0.41–0.95]; P =0.026) (¶24: "tirzepatide reduced the risk of the composite of cardiovascular death or worsening HF events (hazard ratio, 0.62 [95% CI, 0.41–0.95]; P =0.026).")  
  - Change in Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Clinical Summary Score (KCCQ-CSS) at 52 weeks (between-group median difference, 6.9 points [95% CI, 3.3–10.6]; P <0.001) (¶3: "At 52 weeks, tirzepatide increased the Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Clinical Summary Score by 6.9 points (95% CI, 3.3–10.6; P <0.001), 6-minute walk distance 18.3 meters (95% CI, 9.9–26.7; P <0.001), and EQ-5D-5L 0.06 (95% CI, 0.03–0.09; P <0.001).")
