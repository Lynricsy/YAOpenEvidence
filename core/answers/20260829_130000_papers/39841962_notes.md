### Relevance (2)
### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Adults with obesity or overweight with or without diabetes  
- Inclusion criteria: Participants in RCTs evaluating GLP-1 RAs for weight, BMI, and waist circumference outcomes  
- Sample size: 23,244 patients across 47 RCTs  
- Not reported: Age/sex distribution, specific disease/condition beyond obesity/overweight, setting, exclusion criteria  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RAs)  
- Not reported: Specific drugs, dose, frequency, route, duration of intervention  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo  
- Not reported: Specific details of comparator beyond placebo  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes: Mean weight reduction (-4.57 kg, 95% CI -5.35 to -3.78), mean BMI reduction (-2.07 kg/m², 95% CI -2.53 to -1.62), mean waist circumference reduction (-4.55 cm, 95% CI -5.72 to -3.38) compared with placebo  
- Not reported: Cardiovascular outcomes, adverse events, follow-up time  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Systematic review, meta-analysis, and meta-regression of 47 RCTs  
- Not reported: Blinding, randomization details, number of centres, registration, follow-up duration  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- GLP-1 RAs demonstrated a mean weight reduction of -4.57 kg (95% CI -5.35 to -3.78) compared with placebo (¶3: "GLP-1 RAs demonstrated a mean weight reduction of -4.57 kg (95% CI -5.35 to -3.78), mean BMI reduction of -2.07 kg/m2 (95% CI -2.53 to -1.62), and mean waist circumference reduction of -4.55 cm (95% CI -5.72 to -3.38) compared with placebo.")  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Limitations include substantial statistical heterogeneity, in part due to broad inclusion criteria (¶3: "Limitations include substantial statistical heterogeneity, in part due to broad inclusion criteria.")
