### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Participants with symptomatic peripheral artery disease (PAD) and type 2 diabetes (T2D) (¶10: "participants with symptomatic PAD (Fontaine classification IIa) and type 2 diabetes").  
- Inclusion criteria: Age ≥18 years, stable medications, no other non–PAD-related condition limiting functional capacity, no recent cardiovascular event or arterial revascularization (within 180 days), no planned revascularization, and ankle-brachial index ≤0.90 or toe-brachial index ≤0.70 (¶11: "Key inclusion and exclusion criteria were being ≥18 years old, being on stable medications, not having another non–PAD-related condition that limits functional capacity...").  
- Exclusion criteria: Not specified in detail, but excludes those with recent cardiovascular events, revascularization, or planned revascularization (¶11: "neither having a history of a recent cardiovascular event or arterial revascularization (within 180 days) nor a planned revascularization").  
- Age/sex: Age ≥18 years; sex not specified (¶10: "participants with symptomatic PAD (Fontaine classification IIa) and type 2 diabetes").  
- Setting: Multinational trial conducted in 112 sites across 20 countries (¶10: "The trial was multinational, with recruitment conducted in 112 sites across 20 countries").  
- Sample size: 792 participants (¶16: "From 1 October 2020 to 12 July 2024, a total of 792 participants were randomized").

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 1.0 mg (¶10: "once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 1.0 mg to matched placebo").  
- Dose: 1.0 mg (¶10: "once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 1.0 mg").  
- Frequency: Once weekly (¶10: "once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 1.0 mg").  
- Route: Subcutaneous (¶10: "once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 1.0 mg").  
- Duration: 52 weeks (¶11: "primary end point was ratio to baseline at week 52 in maximum walking distance (MWD) on a CLT").

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo (¶10: "once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 1.0 mg to matched placebo").  
- Dose & duration: Placebo matched to semaglutide in frequency and duration (52 weeks) (¶10: "once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 1.0 mg to matched placebo").

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Ratio to baseline (ETR) in maximum walking distance (MWD) at week 52 (¶2: "primary outcome was the ratio to baseline (ETR) in maximum walking distance (MWD)").  
- Secondary outcomes: Pain-free walking distance (PFWD) at week 52 (¶2: "with pain-free walking distance (PFWD) as a key secondary end point").  
- ETR for MWD: 1.14 (95% CI: 1.06–1.22; P = 0.0005) for semaglutide vs. placebo (¶21: "Overall, semaglutide significantly increased MWD with an ETR of 1.14 (95% CI: 1.06–1.22; P = 0.0005) compared with placebo at week 52").  
- ETR for PFWD: 1.15 (95% CI: 1.06–1.24; P = 0.0007) for semaglutide vs. placebo (¶23: "Overall, semaglutide increased PFWD, with an ETR of 1.15 (95% CI: 1.06–1.24; P = 0.0007) among participants who adhered to treatment without initiation of rescue").  
- Follow-up time: 52 weeks (¶11: "primary end point was ratio to baseline at week 52 in maximum walking distance (MWD) on a CLT").  
- Adverse events: Rates of adverse events, serious adverse events, gastrointestinal (GI) disorders, and hypoglycemic events were generally comparable across subgroups (¶31: "The rates of adverse events, serious adverse events, gastrointestinal (GI) disorders, and hypoglycemic events during the on-treatment observation period were generally comparable across subgroups").

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (¶10: "STRIDE was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial").  
- Blinding: Double-blind (¶10: "STRIDE was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial").  
- Randomization: Yes (¶10: "STRIDE was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial").  
- Number of centres: 112 sites across 20 countries (¶10: "recruitment conducted in 112 sites across 20 countries").  
- Registration: Trial registered as NCT04560998 (¶1: "STRIDE trial (NCT04560998)").  
- Follow-up duration: 52 weeks (¶11: "primary end point was ratio to baseline at week 52 in maximum walking distance (MWD) on a CLT").

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Semaglutide significantly improved MWD and PFWD in participants with symptomatic PAD and T2D, regardless of diabetes duration, BMI, HbA1c, or use of SGLT2i or insulin (¶32: "semaglutide significantly improved both MWD and PFWD by week 52, regardless of diabetes duration, BMI, HbA 1c levels, or use of SGLT2i or insulin").  
- The benefits of semaglutide were consistent across subgroups, including those with BMI <30 kg/m² and HbA1c <7% (¶34: "Our results suggest that semaglutide improves functional capacity across a diverse range of patients, including those with well-controlled glycemia (HbA 1c <7%) and a BMI <30 kg/m 2 —groups that are often underrepresented in diabetes trials").  
- The improvements in functional capacity were not strongly correlated with changes in BMI or HbA1c (¶28: "These findings suggest that the benefits of semaglutide for functional capacity may extend beyond its effects on weight loss and glycemic control").  
- Semaglutide’s benefits were independent of SGLT2i or insulin use (¶37: "The benefits of semaglutide were independent of SGLT2i use. Similarly, semaglutide’s effects were consistent regardless of insulin use").

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The STRIDE trial was not designed to assess semaglutide’s interaction with diabetes characteristics, making this a secondary, exploratory analysis (¶39: "The STRIDE trial was not designed to assess semaglutide’s interaction with diabetes characteristics, making this a secondary, exploratory analysis").  
- The analyses may be underpowered to detect interaction effects in smaller subgroups, such as insulin users (¶39: "The analyses may be underpowered to detect interaction effects in smaller subgroups, such as insulin users").  
- No formal adjustment for multiple comparisons was applied, which increases the risk of type I error (¶39: "no formal adjustment for multiple comparisons was applied, which increases the risk of type I error").  
- These findings are hypothesis-generating and warrant confirmation in future adequately powered studies (¶39: "These findings are hypothesis-generating and warrant confirmation in future adequately powered studies").
