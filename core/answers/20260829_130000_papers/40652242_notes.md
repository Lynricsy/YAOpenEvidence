### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: 109,846 patients from 25 unique studies (¶3: "109,846 patients from 25 unique studies were included")  
- Disease/condition: Patients with high atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) risk, including those with and without type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) and/or prior ASCVD (¶4: "GLP-1RA reduced the risk of MI, stroke, cardiovascular mortality and MACE in a broad range of patients with and without T2DM and/or prior ASCVD")  
- Inclusion criteria: Randomized controlled trials (RCTs) relating to treatment with GLP-1RA and prevention of MI (¶15: "This systematic review and meta-analysis was conducted with reference to the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Review and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) 2020. Two electronic databases, Embase and Medline, were searched for randomized controlled trials (RCTs) relating to treatment with GLP-1RA and prevention of MI")  
- Exclusion criteria: Non-randomized controlled trials, observational studies, reviews, study protocols, letters, commentaries, conference abstracts, and studies in pediatric populations (¶16: "Non-randomized controlled trials and observational studies, including cohort and cross-sectional studies, were excluded from this study. Reviews, study protocols, letters, commentaries, conference abstracts were also excluded. Studies in pediatric populations were also excluded in this study")  
- Age/sex: Mean age 62.6 ± 9.2 years; proportion of males ranged from 24 to 76% (¶25: "The proportion of males ranged from 24 to 76%, and the mean age was 62.6 ± 9.2 years")  
- Setting: Most studies were conducted in a multi-national setting, except for two studies conducted only in Japan (¶25: "Most studies were conducted in a multi-national setting, except for two studies conducted only in Japan")  
- Sample size: 109,846 patients (¶3: "109,846 patients from 25 unique studies were included")

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RA) (¶1: "Glucagon like peptide-1 receptor agonist (GLP-1RA) use in individuals with high atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) risk reduces major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE)")  
- Dose: Varies by study (e.g., semaglutide, dulaglutide, liraglutide, exenatide, etc.) (¶25: "Most studies were conducted in patients with T2DM, except for one study conducted in a cohort with obesity in the absence of T2DM")  
- Frequency: Varies by study (e.g., once weekly, daily, etc.) (¶25: "Most studies were conducted in patients with T2DM, except for one study conducted in a cohort with obesity in the absence of T2DM")  
- Route: Varies by study (e.g., subcutaneous injection) (¶25: "Most studies were conducted in patients with T2DM, except for one study conducted in a cohort with obesity in the absence of T2DM")  
- Duration: Follow-up duration of 3.48 ± 1.51 (1.55 to 5.47) years (¶3: "Over a follow-up duration of 3.48 ± 1.51 (1.55 to 5.47) years")

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo, insulin regiments, and other anti-diabetic medications (e.g., metformin, sulfonylurea, SGLT2 inhibitor) (¶25: "Majority of studies compared GLP-1RA to placebo [12, 14, 38–40, 44, 47, 48, 51–61], while others used insulin regiments and other anti-diabetic medications [41–43, 45–47, 49, 50] as the comparators")  
- Dose & duration: Varies by study (e.g., placebo, insulin glargine, etc.) (¶25: "Majority of studies compared GLP-1RA to placebo [12, 14, 38–40, 44, 47, 48, 51–61], while others used insulin regiments and other anti-diabetic medications [41–43, 45–47, 49, 50] as the comparators")

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Myocardial infarction (MI) (RR: 0.86, p < 0.01, NNTB 207) (¶3: "GLP-1RA reduced the risk of total MI (RR: 0.86, p < 0.01), with numbers needed to benefit (NNTB) of 207 to prevent one event of MI")  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - MACE (RR: 0.87, p < 0.01, NNTB 67) (¶3: "GLP-1RA reduced cardiovascular mortality (RR: 0.87, p < 0.01, NNTB 170), MACE (RR: 0.87, p < 0.01, NNTB 67) and stroke (RR: 0.88, p < 0.01, NNTB 335) compared to placebo")  
  - Stroke (RR: 0.88, p < 0.01, NNTB 335) (¶3: "GLP-1RA reduced cardiovascular mortality (RR: 0.87, p < 0.01, NNTB 170), MACE (RR: 0.87, p < 0.01, NNTB 67) and stroke (RR: 0.88, p < 0.01, NNTB 335) compared to placebo")  
  - Cardiovascular mortality (RR: 0.87, p < 0.01, NNTB 170) (¶3: "GLP-1RA reduced cardiovascular mortality (RR: 0.87, p < 0.01, NNTB 170), MACE (RR: 0.87, p < 0.01, NNTB 67) and stroke (RR: 0.88, p < 0.01, NNTB 335) compared to placebo")  
  - Coronary revascularization (RR: 0.85, p = 0.04, NNTB 93) (¶30: "NNTB 93 (95%CI: NNTB 52 to NNTB 1717, Follow-up time: 3.45 ± 1.21 years) for coronary revascularization")  
- Adverse events: Gastrointestinal side-effects (RR: 1.55, p < 0.01, NNTH 9) (¶3: "GLP-1RA commonly resulted in gastrointestinal side-effects amongst other systems (RR: 1.55, p < 0.01, NNTH 9)")  
- Follow-up time: 3.48 ± 1.51 (1.55 to 5.47) years (¶3: "Over a follow-up duration of 3.48 ± 1.51 (1.55 to 5.47) years")
