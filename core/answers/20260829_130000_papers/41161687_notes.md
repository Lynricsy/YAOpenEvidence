### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Adults with obesity  
- Inclusion criteria: Randomised controlled trials (RCTs) on adults with obesity that compared tirzepatide at any dose with placebo, structured lifestyle modification programmes, another anti-obesity medication or other GLP-1RAs or GIP/GLP-1RAs, with a minimum follow-up of six months.  
- Exclusion criteria: Not reported  
- Age: 36.1 to 65.25 years  
- Sex: Not reported  
- Setting: Middle- and high-income countries  
- Sample size: 7111 participants across nine RCTs (n per arm not specified)  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Tirzepatide, a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist  
- Dose: 5 mg to 15 mg  
- Frequency: Once a week  
- Route: Injection  
- Duration: Medium-term (12 to 18 months) and long-term (3.5 years)  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo, structured lifestyle modification programmes, another anti-obesity medication or other GLP-1RAs or GIP/GLP-1RAs  
- Dose & duration: Placebo comparisons were conducted with tirzepatide at various doses (5 mg to 15 mg) over medium-term (12 to 18 months) and long-term (3.5 years) durations  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes: Weight reduction, adverse events, major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE), quality of life, and mortality  
- Secondary outcomes: Waist circumference and other obesity-related comorbidities  
- Weight reduction:  
  - Medium-term: Mean difference (MD) -16.03 (95% CI -18.91 to -13.14) in percentage reduction in body weight from baseline (8 studies, 6317 participants; moderate-certainty evidence)  
  - Long-term: MD -15.66 (95% CI -19.14 to -12.18) in percentage reduction in body weight from baseline (1 study, 1032 participants; moderate-certainty evidence)  
- 5% weight reduction:  
  - Medium-term: Risk ratio (RR) 3.60 (95% CI 2.44 to 5.30) (5 studies, 4455 participants; moderate-certainty evidence)  
  - Long-term: RR 2.81 (95% CI 2.33 to 3.38) (1 study, 1032 participants; moderate-certainty evidence)  
- Adverse events:  
  - Non-serious adverse events: RR 1.33 (95% CI 1.03 to 1.71) (5 studies, 4582 participants; low-certainty evidence)  
  - Serious adverse events: RR 0.99 (95% CI 0.88 to 1.12) (8 studies, 6359 participants; very low-certainty evidence)  
- Adverse events leading to withdrawal:  
  - Medium-term: RR 2.06 (95% CI 1.21 to 3.52) (8 studies, 6359 participants; low-certainty evidence)  
  - Long-term: RR 1.64 (95% CI 0.97 to 2.76) (1 study, 1032 participants; low-certainty evidence)  
- MACE:  
  - Medium-term: RR 0.75 (95% CI 0.34 to 1.66) (7 studies, 5628 participants; moderate-certainty evidence)  
  - Long-term: RR 0.56 (95% CI 0.22 to 1.42) (1 study, 1032 participants; moderate-certainty evidence)  
- Quality of life:  
  - Medium-term: MD 9.91 (95% CI 7.81 to 12.02) in IWQOL-Lite-CT physical function domain (6 studies, 5020 participants; moderate-certainty evidence)  
  - Long-term: MD 2.70 (95% CI 0.00 to 5.40) in SF-36 physical component score (1 study, 1032 participants; low-certainty evidence)  
- Mortality:  
  - Medium-term: RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.34 to 1.83) (7 studies, 5628 participants; moderate-certainty evidence)  
  - Long-term: RR 0.83 (95% CI 0.22 to 3.17) (1 study, 1032 participants; low-certainty evidence)  
- Follow-up time: Medium-term (12 to 18 months) and long-term (3.5 years)  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs  
- Blinding: Not explicitly reported  
- Randomization: Included RCTs  
- Number of centres: Not reported  
- Registration: Protocol registered with PROSPERO (CRD420250654193)  
- Follow-up duration: Medium-term (12 to 18 months) and long-term (3.5 years)  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Tirzepatide likely results in little to no difference in MACE compared to placebo (RR 0.75, 95% CI 0.34 to 1.66; I² = 0%; 7 studies, 5628 participants; moderate-certainty evidence) (¶9: "Tirzepatide likely results in little to no difference in MACE (RR 0.75, 95% CI 0.34 to 1.66; I² = 0%; 7 studies, 5628 participants; moderate-certainty evidence).")  
- Tirzepatide likely results in little to no difference in MACE at long-term follow-up (RR 0.56, 95% CI 0.22 to 1.42; 1 study, 1032 participants; moderate-certainty evidence) (¶11: "Tirzepatide likely results in little to no difference in MACE (RR 0.56, 95% CI 0.22 to 1.42; 1 study, 1032 participants; moderate-certainty evidence).")  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The certainty of the evidence on events leading to withdrawal at medium- and long-term follow-up is low, which might further limit our understanding of the sustainability of the initial effects (¶12: "The certainty of the evidence on events leading to withdrawal at medium- and long-term follow-up is low, which might further limit our understanding of the sustainability of the initial effects.")  
- All the included studies were funded by the drug manufacturer, raising concerns about potential conflicts of interest (¶12: "All the included studies were funded by the drug manufacturer, raising concerns about potential conflicts of interest.")
