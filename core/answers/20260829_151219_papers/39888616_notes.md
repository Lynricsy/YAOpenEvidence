### Relevance (1)
### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Adults with overweight or obesity (BMI ≥27) initiating GLP-1 RAs (liraglutide, injectable semaglutide, or tirzepatide) between January 1, 2018, and December 31, 2023 (¶13: "We identified adults with overweight or obesity first dispensed a GLP-1 RA between January 1, 2018, and December 31, 2023.")  
- Inclusion criteria: BMI ≥27, weight measured in the 60 days preceding the first dispensing of a GLP-1 RA, regular interactions with the health care system in the previous year, and a prescription for a GLP-1 RA in the preceding 2 months (¶13: "We required a BMI of 27 or higher and a weight measured in the 60 days preceding the first dispensing of a GLP-1 RA.")  
- Exclusion criteria: Patients with a history of type 1 diabetes, gestational diabetes, or diabetic retinopathy, those with missing data on sex, or no follow-up encounters after GLP-1 RA initiation (¶13: "Patients with a history of type 1 diabetes, gestational diabetes, or diabetic retinopathy, as well as those with missing data on sex or with no follow-up encounters after GLP-1 RA initiation were excluded.")  
- Age/sex: Mean (SD) age overall was 54.4 (13.1) years; 65.4% female, 34.6% male (¶21: "The mean (SD) age overall was 54.4 (13.1) years; 82 063 patients (65.4%) were female, and 43 411 patients (34.6%) were male;")  
- Setting: Retrospective observational cohort study using electronic health record (EHR) data from 30 US health care systems (¶10: "This study used a subset of Truveta Data. Truveta provides access to continuously updated and linked electronic health record (EHR) data from a collective of 30 US health care systems.")  
- Sample size: 125,474 patients (¶21: "Overall, 125 474 adults met inclusion criteria, including 76 524 patients (61.0%) with type 2 diabetes and 48 950 patients (39.0%) without type 2 diabetes.")

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Initiation of GLP-1 RAs (liraglutide, injectable semaglutide, or tirzepatide) (¶13: "New users of liraglutide, injectable semaglutide, or tirzepatide (henceforth referred to as GLP-1 RAs) with overweight or obesity (regardless of type 2 diabetes) between January 1, 2018, and December 31, 2023, were included.")  
- Dose, frequency, route, duration: Not reported in the text.

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Not reported in the text.

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes: Discontinuation and reinitiation of GLP-1 RAs within 1 and 2 years (¶2: "In this cohort study of 125 474 patients initiating GLP-1 RAs, 46.5% of patients with and 64.8% without type 2 diabetes discontinued within 1 year; 47.3% of patients with and 36.3% without type 2 diabetes subsequently reinitiated a GLP-1 RA within 1 year.")  
- Secondary outcomes: Factors associated with discontinuation and reinitiation, including weight loss, income, adverse events, and weight regain (¶2: "Weight loss, income, and adverse events were significantly associated with discontinuation, while weight regain was significantly associated with reinitiation.")  
- Follow-up time: Up to 2 years (¶8: "Patients were followed up for up to 2 years to assess discontinuation.")  
- Adverse events: Moderate or severe GI adverse events (e.g., bowel obstruction, cholecystitis, cholelithiasis, gastroenteritis, gastroparesis, or pancreatitis) were associated with discontinuation (¶27: "On-treatment moderate or severe incident GI adverse events were associated with a higher hazard of discontinuation for patients with type 2 diabetes (HR, 1.38 [95% CI, 1.31-1.45]) and for patients without type 2 diabetes (HR, 1.19 [95% CI, 1.12-1.27]).")  
- Statistical results:  
  - Discontinuation rates: 53.6% (95% CI, 53.4%-53.9%) by 1 year, 72.2% (95% CI, 71.9%-72.4%) by 2 years (¶24: "Accounting for censoring, 53.6% (95% CI, 53.4%-53.9%) discontinued by 1 year, and 72.2% (95% CI, 71.9%-72.4%) discontinued by 2 years.")  
  - Reinitiation rates: 47.3% (95% CI, 46.6%-48.0%) of patients with type 2 diabetes and 36.3% (95% CI, 35.6%-37.0%) of patients without type 2 diabetes reinitiated within 1 year (¶31: "Accounting for censoring, 47.3% (95% CI, 46.6%-48.0%) of patients with type 2 diabetes and 36.3% (95% CI, 35.6%-37.0%) of patients without type 2 diabetes reinitiated a GLP-1 RA within 1 year.")

### S — Study design / 研究设计

- Blinding: Not reported in the text.

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Discontinuation rates were significantly higher for patients without type 2 diabetes (64.8% within 1 year) compared to those with type 2 diabetes (46.5% within 1 year) (¶2: "In this cohort study of 125 474 patients initiating GLP-1 RAs, 46.5% of patients with and 64.8% without type 2 diabetes discontinued within 1 year;")  
- Reinitiation rates were significantly lower for patients without type 2 diabetes (36.3% within 1 year) compared to those with type 2 diabetes (47.3% within 1 year) (¶2: "47.3% of patients with and 36.3% without type 2 diabetes subsequently reinitiated a GLP-1 RA within 1 year.")  
- Greater on-treatment weight loss was associated with a lower likelihood of discontinuation (a 1% reduction in weight from baseline was associated with a 3.1% lower hazard of discontinuation for patients with type 2 diabetes and 3.3% lower hazard for those without type 2 diabetes) (¶26: "A 1% reduction in weight from baseline was associated with a 3.1% (95% CI, 2.9%-3.2%) lower hazard of discontinuation for patients with type 2 diabetes and a 3.3% (95% CI, 3.2%-3.5%) lower hazard of discontinuation for patients without type 2 diabetes.")
