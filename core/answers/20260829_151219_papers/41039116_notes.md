### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Adults with obesity  
- Disease/condition: Obesity, with related complications such as type 2 diabetes, metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH), obstructive sleep apnea syndrome (OSAS), and cardiovascular disease (CVD)  
- Inclusion criteria: Not explicitly stated, but trials included patients with obesity (no studies reported a mean baseline BMI < 30 kg/m²)  
- Exclusion criteria: Not explicitly stated  
- Age/sex: Not explicitly stated  
- Setting: Clinical trials (RCTs)  
- Sample size: 60,307 patients (32,598 OMM and 27,709 placebo)  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Tirzepatide: Used in 6 trials  
- Semaglutide: Used in 14 trials  
- Other OMMs: Orlistat (22 trials), liraglutide (11 trials), naltrexone/bupropion (5 trials), phentermine/topiramate (2 trials)  
- Dose, frequency, route, duration: Not explicitly stated for tirzepatide and semaglutide in the text  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Placebo: All OMMs were compared to placebo in 58 out of 60 comparisons  
- Active control: Two head-to-head comparisons: liraglutide vs. orlistat and semaglutide vs. liraglutide  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- **Primary outcome**: Percentage of total body weight loss (TBWL%) at the end of the study  
  - Semaglutide: 11.9% (8.1–15.7)  
  - Tirzepatide: 16.2% (11.2–21.3)  
- **Secondary outcomes**:  
  - TBWL% at 52 weeks: Semaglutide 12.1% (9.0–15.2), Tirzepatide 14.8% (11.7–17.9)  
  - TBWL% at 53–104 weeks: Semaglutide 12.5% (8.3–16.8), Tirzepatide 16.9% (15.9–17.9)  
  - Waist circumference reduction: Semaglutide −8.4 cm (−11.5 to −5.2), Tirzepatide −11.2 cm (−14.6 to −7.7)  
  - BMI reduction: Semaglutide −4.0 kg/m² (−5.4 to −2.6), Tirzepatide −5.1 kg/m² (−8.0 to −2.3)  
  - Proportion of patients achieving ≥25% TBWL: Tirzepatide 33.8% (18.4–61.9)  
  - Cardiovascular outcomes:  
    - MACE: Semaglutide 0.8 (0.4–7.3), Tirzepatide 0.8 (0.3–1.8)  
    - Cardiovascular mortality: Semaglutide 0.8 (0.7–1.0), Tirzepatide 1.2 (0.5–3.0)  
    - Hospitalization due to heart failure (HHF): Tirzepatide 0.4 (0.2–0.9), Semaglutide 0.4 (0.2–1.0)  
  - Adverse events:  
    - All-cause mortality: Semaglutide 0.8 (0.7–0.9), Tirzepatide 1.1 (0.6–1.9)  
    - Serious adverse events (SAEs): Semaglutide 0.9 (0.2–4.9), Tirzepatide 0.3 (0.1–1.3)  
- Follow-up time: Varies, with data up to more than 156 weeks  
- Adverse events: Not explicitly detailed for tirzepatide and semaglutide in the text  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study type: Systematic review and network meta-analysis (NMA)  
- Blinding: Most RCTs were double-blind (66%), with some reporting inadequate blinding (29%)  
- Randomization: All RCTs were randomized  
- Number of centres: Not explicitly stated  
- Registration: Not explicitly stated  
- Follow-up duration: Varies, with data up to more than 156 weeks  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Tirzepatide and semaglutide showed significantly greater TBWL% compared to placebo, with tirzepatide achieving the highest TBWL% (16.2%) (¶12: "Semaglutide Tirzepatide Body weight TBWL (WMD, %) Endpoint (overall) 3.1 [0.7,5.5] 4.8 [3.7,5.8] 4.5 [1.0,7.9] 8.8 [8.2,9.5] 11.9 [8.1,15.7] 16.2 [11.2,21.3]")  
- Both tirzepatide and semaglutide showed normoglycemia restoration, remission of type 2 diabetes, and reduction in hospitalization due to heart failure (¶2: "Both tirzepatide and semaglutide showed normoglycemia restoration, remission of type 2 diabetes and reduction in hospitalization due to heart failure.")  
- Tirzepatide was associated with a greater reduction in waist circumference and BMI compared to semaglutide (¶12: "Waist circumference (WMD, cm) −0.9 [−3.7,1.8] −1.4 [−4.5,1.8] −3.2 [−5.6,−0.8] −6.9 [−7.6,−5.9] −8.4 [−11.5,−5.2] −11.2 [−14.6,−7.7]")  
- Semaglutide was associated with a significantly lower risk of MACE compared to other OMMs (¶30: "Only semaglutide was associated with a significantly lower risk for MACE, with all OMMs reporting odds ratios below 1, except for phentermine plus topiramate (odds ratio = 2.00 [0.18–22.1], P > 0.50")  
- Tirzepatide was associated with a significantly lower risk of HHF compared to other OMMs (¶30: "HHF was significantly reduced by tirzepatide (n = 2 RCTs), whereas semaglutide (n = 4 RCTs) showed a non-significant trend toward a reduction.")  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The availability of head-to-head comparisons between different OMMs in RCTs is still limited (¶5: "However, the availability of head-to-head comparisons between different OMMs in randomized controlled trials (RCTs) is still limited")  
- The quality of studies was heterogeneous (¶9: "The quality of studies was heterogeneous (Supplementary Fig. 2)")  
- Most of the included RCTs were double blind, but a few trials inadequately reported attrition and/or description of allocation or blinding of assessors (¶9: "Most of the included RCTs were double blind (66%), with a few trials inadequately reporting attrition and/or description of allocation or blinding of assessors (29%)")  
- No studies reported a mean baseline BMI < 30 kg/m² (¶8: "No studies reported a mean baseline body mass index (BMI) < 30 kg m − 2")
