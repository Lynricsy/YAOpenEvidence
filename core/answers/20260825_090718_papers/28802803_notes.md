### Study design  
Type: Systematic review and meta-analysis  
Population: Humans (diabetic and non-diabetic populations)  
n: 53 studies included in the analysis  
Intervention/Comparator: Metformin compared to non-diabetic populations and diabetics receiving other therapies (non-metformin therapies, insulin, sulphonylurea)  
Follow-up: Not explicitly stated, but studies included were those investigating the effect of metformin on all-cause mortality or diseases of ageing.

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Diabetics taking metformin had significantly lower all-cause mortality than non-diabetics (HR=0.93, 95%CI 0.88–0.99).  
- Diabetics taking metformin had reduced cardiovascular disease (CVD) compared to diabetics receiving non-metformin therapies (HR=0.76, 95%CI 0.66–0.87) or insulin (HR=0.78, 95%CI 0.73–0.83).  
- The study suggests that metformin may have a geroprotective effect, reducing mortality and diseases of ageing independent of its effect on diabetes control.  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Differences in baseline characteristics between groups were observed, which had the potential to bias findings, although statistical adjustments were made.
