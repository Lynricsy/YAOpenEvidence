### Relevance (1)
### Study design  
The paper is a narrative review discussing the potential uses of metformin beyond type 2 diabetes. It does not present a specific study design with a defined population, intervention, or follow-up period. Instead, it synthesizes pre-clinical and clinical data from various studies.

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- The paper discusses that metformin may protect against neurodegenerative diseases via suppression of pro-inflammatory pathways, protection of mitochondria and vascular function, and direct actions on neuronal stem cells.
- It mentions that metformin may improve vascular endothelial function independently of its effects on glucose homeostasis, which could have cardiovascular implications.
- However, the paper does not specifically report on cardiovascular outcomes in non-diabetic populations.

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- It remains unclear whether all of the putative beneficial effects of metformin are secondary to its actions as an anti-hyperglycemic and insulin-sensitizing drug or result from other cellular actions.
- The reproducibility of the data is questioned.
- Data from ex vivo studies using high concentrations of metformin may not translate into clinical benefits and may reflect a "Paracelsus" effect (i.e., the dose makes the poison).
