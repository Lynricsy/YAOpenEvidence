### Study design  
Type: Systematic review of randomized controlled trials (RCTs)  
Population: Patients with or without type 2 diabetes (T2D)  
n: 2486 patients across nine studies  
Intervention/Comparator: Metformin vs. placebo  
Follow-up: Not explicitly stated, but outcomes related to heart failure markers were assessed

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Metformin did not affect left ventricular function.  
- Metformin improved myocardial oxygen consumption.  
- Metformin reduced markers of heart failure such as n-terminal pro-brain natriuretic peptide (NT-proBNP) and low-density lipoprotein (LDL) levels, though this was inconsistent between diabetic and non-diabetic patients.  
- The beneficial effects of metformin were linked to its ability to ameliorate insulin resistance and block pro-inflammatory markers such as CCL11.  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The evidence on metformin's impact on heart failure-related outcomes remains inconclusive.  
- The results regarding heart failure markers were inconsistent between diabetic and non-diabetic patients.  
- Additional RCTs are necessary to improve understanding of metformin’s modulatory effects on heart failure-related outcomes, especially in diabetic patients.
