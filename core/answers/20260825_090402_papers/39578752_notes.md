### Relevance
2

### Study design  
Type: Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs  
Population: Patients with chronic heart failure (HF), regardless of HF type and glycemic status  
n: Not explicitly stated, but based on pooled RCT data  
Intervention/Comparator: Empagliflozin (EMPA) vs. control  
Follow-up: Long-term (duration of therapy ≥6 months was analyzed as a subgroup)

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- EMPA was **less effective for improving quality of life (QoL)** in HFpEF populations.  
- The **benefits of EMPA on cardiac remodeling and function** were **more pronounced in HFpEF** compared to other subgroups (e.g., HFrEF, absence of diabetes, and treatment duration ≥6 months).  
- No specific **reduction in cardiovascular mortality or hospitalization for HF** was reported for HFpEF patients in this study.  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Data concerning the long-term cardiovascular effects in clinically important subgroups (including HFpEF) are scarce.  
- The study did not report specific outcomes for HFpEF patients in terms of cardiovascular mortality or hospitalization for HF.  
- The analysis was based on pooled RCT data, which may not capture real-world variability.
