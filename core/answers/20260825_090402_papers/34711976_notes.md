### Study design  
- **Type**: Multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial  
- **Population**: 324 patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF), including those with and without type 2 diabetes (T2D)  
- **n**: 324 patients (162 in dapagliflozin group, 162 in placebo group)  
- **Intervention/Comparator**: Dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily vs. placebo  
- **Follow-up**: 12 weeks  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Dapagliflozin significantly improved the **Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Clinical Summary Score (KCCQ-CS)** by **5.8 points (95% CI 2.3–9.2, P = 0.001)** at 12 weeks, meeting the primary endpoint.  
- This improvement was driven by **improvements in KCCQ total symptom score (5.8 points, 95% CI 2.0–9.6, P = 0.003)** and **physical limitations score (5.3 points, 95% CI 0.7–10.0, P = 0.026)**.  
- Dapagliflozin improved **6-minute walk test (6MWT)** by **20.1 m (95% CI 5.6–34.7, P = 0.007)**.  
- **KCCQ-OS** improved by **4.5 points (95% CI 1.1–7.8, P = 0.009)**.  
- A higher proportion of dapagliflozin-treated patients had **≥5-point improvement in KCCQ-OS** (OR = 1.73, 95% CI 1.05–2.85, P = 0.03).  
- Dapagliflozin reduced **weight** by **0.72 kg (95% CI 0.01–1.42, P = 0.046)**.  
- Benefits were consistent across subgroups, including patients with and without T2D and those with ejection fraction above and below 60%.  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The **relatively short duration of follow-up** (12 weeks) precludes assessment of the durability of the observed benefits on HF-disease-specific symptoms or functional status.  
- All patients were enrolled at **sites in the United States**, which may limit the generalizability of findings to other populations.  
- The study did not assess long-term outcomes such as **hospitalization rates or mortality**, which are important in HFpEF.
