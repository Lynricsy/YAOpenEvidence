### Study design  
- **Type**: Systematic review and meta-analysis  
- **Population**: Patients with heart failure with mildly reduced and preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF and HFrEF)  
- **n**: Multiple trials included (DECLARE-TIMI, DAPA-HF, DELIVER, EMPEROR trials)  
- **Intervention/Comparator**: SGLT2 inhibitors (e.g., dapagliflozin, empagliflozin) vs. placebo or standard care  
- **Follow-up**: Varies by trial (e.g., DECLARE-TIMI: 4.2 years; DAPA-HF: 18.2 months; DELIVER: up to 33 months)

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- **Dapagliflozin in DECLARE-TIMI trial**:  
  - Reduced hospitalization for heart failure (HHF) in patients with HFpEF (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.62–0.94).  
  - No significant reduction in cardiovascular or all-cause mortality in HFpEF patients.  
- **DAPA-HF trial (HFrEF only)**:  
  - Reduced cardiovascular death (HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.69–0.98) and HHF (HR 0.70, 95% CI 0.59–0.83).  
- **DELIVER trial (HFpEF)**:  
  - Ongoing trial evaluating dapagliflozin’s impact on cardiovascular death, HHF, or urgent heart failure visits in HFpEF patients.  
  - Expected to provide definitive evidence on the benefits of SGLT2 inhibitors in HFpEF.  
- **Meta-analysis of CVOTs**:  
  - SGLT2 inhibitors reduced 3-point MACE (HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.82–0.94), cardiovascular death (HR 0.83, 95% CI 0.75–0.92), and all-cause mortality (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.79–0.92).  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Limited data on SGLT2 inhibitors in HFpEF patients, with most trials focusing on HFrEF.  
- The DECLARE-TIMI trial had limited power to detect effects in HFpEF due to a relatively small cohort (n = 1316).  
- The DELIVER trial results are pending and not yet reported.  
- Variability in definitions of HFpEF and HFrEF across trials may affect comparability.  
- Lack of long-term follow-up data on cardiovascular and renal outcomes in HFpEF.
