### Study design  
The paper is a review/analysis discussing the potential mechanisms of SGLT2 inhibitors in HFpEF, based on clinical trials (e.g., EMPEROR-Preserved, DELIVER) and mechanistic/imaging studies. It does not present a new clinical trial but synthesizes existing evidence and explores underlying pathophysiological mechanisms.

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduce hospitalizations for HFpEF and are now recommended as foundational therapy in established HFpEF populations (as shown in trials like EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER).  
- SGLT2 inhibitors preferentially remove interstitial fluid while preserving plasma volume, potentially improving diastolic function and alleviating pulmonary and renal congestion.  
- The benefits of SGLT2 inhibitors may be enhanced when combined with loop or thiazide diuretics in patients with overt congestion.  
- Interstitial fluid retention is a key mechanism in HFpEF, and SGLT2 inhibitors target this mechanism, improving outcomes through integrated modulation of hemodynamic, renal, and molecular pathways.

### Limitations stated by the authors  
The authors note that the precise mechanisms underlying the benefits of SGLT2 inhibitors in HFpEF remain incompletely understood. Additionally, HFpEF is a heterogeneous syndrome with multiple phenotypes, and the generalizability of findings may vary depending on the specific patient subgroup.
