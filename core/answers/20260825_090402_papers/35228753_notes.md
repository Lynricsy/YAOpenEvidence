### Study design  
- **Type**: Randomized, double-blind trial  
- **Population**: 476 participants with heart failure (HF), regardless of ejection fraction (EF) or diabetes status  
- **n**: 476  
- **Intervention/Comparator**: Canagliflozin (100 mg) vs. placebo  
- **Follow-up**: 12 weeks  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- The 12-week change in Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Total Symptom Score (KCCQ TSS) was **4.3 points (95% confidence interval, 0.8–7.8; P = 0.016)** higher with canagliflozin than with placebo, meeting the primary endpoint.  
- Similar effects were observed in participants with **HFpEF** and in those with **HFrEF**, as well as in participants with and without diabetes.  
- Canagliflozin significantly improves **symptom burden in HF**, regardless of EF or diabetes status.  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Enrollment was stopped early due to **shifting sponsor priorities**, without unblinding.  
- The study was conducted in a **remote, all-virtual format**, which may have influenced participant adherence or data collection.
