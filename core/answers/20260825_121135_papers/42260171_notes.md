### Study design  
- **Type**: Subgroup analysis of a large-scale meta-analysis  
- **Population**: 56,580 patients from 45 studies (mixed, intention-to-treat [ITT], and treatment-adherent populations)  
- **n**: 56,580 patients  
- **Intervention/Comparator**: GLP-1 receptor agonists (including semaglutide and liraglutide) versus placebo  
- **Follow-up**: Long-term weight-loss outcomes (up to 12 months)  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Non-diabetic participants achieved the greatest weight loss, losing **15.7% of baseline weight at 12 months** versus **5.1% in diabetic users** in the mixed cohort.  
- **Semaglutide produced significantly greater reductions than liraglutide** after adjustment (β = +1.67, p < 0.001).  
- **Diabetes status was a strong effect modifier**: non-diabetic individuals achieved **2.5–4× greater reductions** than diabetics receiving the same agents.  
- **Meta-regression confirmed diabetes status as an independent negative predictor of weight loss** (β = −1.77, p < 0.001).  
- **Baseline BMI was inversely associated with percent weight change** (β = +0.46, p < 0.001).
