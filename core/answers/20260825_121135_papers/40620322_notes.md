### Study design  
The paper is a review article summarizing data from multiple clinical trials (SUSTAIN, PIONEER, STEP, and FOCUS) and discusses the therapeutic role of semaglutide in various conditions. It does not describe a single study with a defined population, intervention, or follow-up period, but rather synthesizes findings from multiple trials.

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- The STEP trials highlight semaglutide's effectiveness in weight control, with participants achieving up to **20% weight loss** alongside improvements in obesity-related comorbidities.  
- Long-term studies affirm the **overall safety and tolerability** of semaglutide.  
- The most commonly reported adverse events are **gastrointestinal side effects**.  
- Semaglutide exhibits **renoprotective, anti-inflammatory, and potential hepatoprotective effects**.  
- The availability of both **subcutaneous and oral formulations** enhances treatment adherence and accessibility.

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- The authors note that future research should continue to explore semaglutide's therapeutic potential in **under-studied populations**, including individuals with **non-diabetic cardiovascular disease** and **hepatic fibrosis**.
