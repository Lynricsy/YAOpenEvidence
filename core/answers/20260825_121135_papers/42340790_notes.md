### Study design  
Type: Systematic review and meta-analysis  
Population: Non-diabetic patients with obesity and chronic kidney disease (CKD) or hypertension  
n: 430 participants across 3 studies (1 RCT, 1 real-world observational dialysis study, 1 post hoc trial analysis)  
Intervention/Comparator: Semaglutide vs. standard care or placebo (not explicitly stated in the abstract)  
Follow-up: Not explicitly stated, but results are based on pooled data from multiple studies, including long-term outcomes.

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Semaglutide significantly reduced BMI in non-diabetic obese patients:  
  - Tuttle study: 18.3% mean reduction  
  - Apperloo study: 11.8% mean reduction  
  - Vanek study: 1.5% mean reduction  
  - P = 0.001 for overall effect  
- Improved hypertension control:  
  - Proportion of patients with SBP <130 mmHg:  
    - Apperloo: 20.6%  
    - Vanek: 12.5%  
    - Tuttle: 17.6%  
    - P < 0.02 for overall effect  
- Improved renal function:  
  - Increase in eGFR >30 ml/min/1.73m² from baseline:  
    - Apperloo: 65.8%  
    - Vanek: 4.83%  
    - Tuttle: 36.4%  
    - P < 0.001 for overall effect  
- No publication bias detected.

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Limited number of eligible studies (only 3 studies included)  
- Heterogeneity in study designs (RCT, observational, post hoc analysis)  
- Potential for residual confounding due to observational study components  
- Lack of long-term safety data beyond the included studies
