### Relevance (2)
### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Individuals with obesity (BMI 32-43) aged 18-65 years (¶8: "Inclusion criteria were age (18-65 years), obesity (body mass index [BMI] of 32-43 [calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared]), and safe contraceptive method or postmenopausal status.")  
- Inclusion criteria: Age 18-65 years, BMI 32-43, safe contraceptive method or postmenopausal status (¶8: "Inclusion criteria were age (18-65 years), obesity (body mass index [BMI] of 32-43 [calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared]), and safe contraceptive method or postmenopausal status.")  
- Exclusion criteria: Any known serious chronic illness, including diabetes (¶8: "Exclusion criteria were any known serious chronic illness, including diabetes.")  
- Setting: Hvidovre Hospital and the Department of Biomedical Sciences of the University of Copenhagen, Denmark (¶7: "The study was conducted from August 2016 to November 2019 at Hvidovre Hospital and the Department of Biomedical Sciences of the University of Copenhagen, Denmark.")  
- Sample size: 195 participants randomized to 4 groups (exercise group, liraglutide group, combination group, placebo group) (¶20: "A total of 195 participants [...] were randomized to the exercise group (48 participants), liraglutide group (49 participants), combination group (49 participants), or placebo group (49 participants).")

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention:  
  - Exercise: 2 group sessions per week (indoor cycling and circuit training) and 2 individual sessions per week (moderate-to-vigorous intensity) (¶11: "Participants were encouraged to attend group exercise sessions 2 times per week and to perform exercise individually 2 times per week.")  
  - Liraglutide: Once daily subcutaneous injections, starting at 0.6 mg/day with weekly increments to 3.0 mg/day (¶13: "Liraglutide or volume-matched placebo was administered as once daily subcutaneous injections in the abdomen. The starting dose was 0.6 mg/day with weekly increments of 0.6 mg until reaching a dose of 3.0 mg/day.")  
  - Combination: Exercise + liraglutide (¶10: "Participants who lost at least 5% of their initial body weight were then randomly assigned to a 1-year intervention phase with either exercise and placebo (exercise group), liraglutide (liraglutide group), a combination of exercise and liraglutide (combination group), or placebo (placebo group).")  
- Duration: 1-year intervention phase following an 8-week low-calorie diet (¶10: "Participants who lost at least 5% of their initial body weight were then randomly assigned to a 1-year intervention phase [...]")

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo (volume-matched to liraglutide) (¶10: "Participants who lost at least 5% of their initial body weight were then randomly assigned to a 1-year intervention phase with either exercise and placebo (exercise group), liraglutide (liraglutide group), a combination of exercise and liraglutide (combination group), or placebo (placebo group).")  
- Duration: 1-year intervention phase (same as the other groups) (¶10: "Participants who lost at least 5% of their initial body weight were then randomly assigned to a 1-year intervention phase [...]")

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes:  
  - Changes in BMD at the hip, lumbar spine, and distal forearm (¶15: "BMD in grams per centimeter squared was measured with DXA in fasting state separately at the left hip and lumbar spine (L1-L4), which are considered the important sites for assessments of fracture risk and treatment response, respectively.")  
  - Results:  
    - Combination group: No significant change in hip BMD (mean change, −0.006 g/cm²; 95% CI, −0.017 to 0.004 g/cm²; P = .24) or lumbar spine BMD (mean change, −0.010 g/cm²; 95% CI, −0.025 to 0.005 g/cm²; P = .20) compared with placebo (¶25: "In the combination group, BMD was unchanged compared with the placebo group at the hip [...] P = .24) and lumbar spine [...] P = .20).")  
    - Liraglutide group: Decreased hip BMD compared with exercise group (mean change, −0.013 g/cm²; 95% CI, −0.024 to −0.001 g/cm²; P = .03) and placebo group (mean change, −0.013 g/cm²; 95% CI, −0.024 to −0.002 g/cm²; P = .02) (¶25: "In the liraglutide group, hip BMD decreased compared with the exercise group [...] P = .03) and placebo group [...] P = .02).")  
    - Liraglutide group: Decreased lumbar spine BMD compared with exercise group (mean change, −0.016 g/cm²; 95% CI, −0.032 to −0.001 g/cm²; P = .04) and placebo group (mean change, −0.019 g/cm²; 95% CI, −0.034 to 0.002 g/cm²; P = .02) (¶25: "Likewise, in the liraglutide group, lumbar spine BMD decreased compared with the exercise group [...] P = .04) and placebo group [...] P = .02).")  
    - Exercise and combination groups: Increased distal forearm BMD (no differences between groups) (¶25: "Distal forearm BMD increased in the exercise and combination groups, with no differences between the 4 groups regarding distal forearm BMD.")  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - Whole-body BMD: Liraglutide and combination treatments were associated with increased whole-body BMD (eFigure 2 in Supplement 2) (¶26: "Compared with placebo, the liraglutide and combination treatments were associated with increased whole-body BMD (eFigure 2 in Supplement 2 ).")  
  - Bone turnover markers: P-CTX increased by 27% (95% CI, 21%-35%) during the low-calorie diet and decreased in all treatment groups by week 26 (lowest in placebo group). P-P1NP increased by 7% (95% CI, 4%-10%) during the low-calorie diet and returned to initial levels by week 52 (¶29: "In response to the 8-week low-calorie diet, P-CTX increased by a mean of 27% [...] P-P1NP increased by a mean of 7% [...] at week 52, P1NP had returned to the initial levels in all 4 groups.")  
- Follow-up time: 52 weeks (1 year) after randomization (¶15: "Changes in BMD were calculated from before the low-calorie diet to 1 year after randomization.")  
- Adverse events: Not reported in this paper (Not reported)

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Randomized clinical trial (RCT) (¶7: "This study is a predefined secondary analysis of a randomized clinical trial [...]")  
- Blinding: Participants, personnel, and investigators were blinded regarding study medication until the primary outcomes were analyzed (¶9: "Participants, personnel, and investigators were blinded regarding study medication until the primary outcomes were analyzed.")  
- Randomization: 1:1:1:1 ratio stratified by sex and age (<40 years or ≥40 years) (¶9: "Participants were randomized [...] in a 1:1:1:1 ratio stratified by sex and age (<40 years or ≥40 years).")  
- Number of centres: 2 (Hvidovre Hospital and the Department of Biomedical Sciences of the University of Copenhagen, Denmark) (¶7: "The study was conducted [...] at Hvidovre Hospital and the Department of Biomedical Sciences of the University of Copenhagen, Denmark.")  
- Registration: Not reported (Not reported)  
- Follow-up duration: 52 weeks (1 year) after randomization (¶15: "Changes in BMD were calculated from before the low-calorie diet to 1 year after randomization.")
