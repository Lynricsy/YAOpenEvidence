### Relevance (1)
### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Patients with obesity and/or type 2 diabetes (T2D) who were new users of GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 agonists) (¶4: "Patients 18 years or older were included if their index date (first-recorded GLP-1 agonist fill of dulaglutide, exenatide, liraglutide, or semaglutide) was in 2021.")  
- Inclusion criteria: Age ≥18 years, first GLP-1 agonist fill in 2021, continuous enrollment in a commercial, Medicare, or Medicaid plan or dual Medicare-Medicaid eligibility for at least 12 months before and 17 months after the index date (¶4: "Patients 18 years or older were included if their index date (first-recorded GLP-1 agonist fill of dulaglutide, exenatide, liraglutide, or semaglutide) was in 2021. Other inclusion requirements were continuous enrollment in a commercial, Medicare, or Medicaid plan or dual Medicare-Medicaid eligibility for at least 12 months before (baseline) and at least 17 months after (follow-up) the index date.")  
- Exclusion criteria: Patients without T2D or obesity at baseline (¶4: "Patients without T2D or obesity at baseline were excluded.")  
- Sample size: 195,915 individuals (¶7: "The study included 195 915 individuals (mean [SD] age, 53.8 [12.5] years; 115 394 females [58.9%], 80 521 males [41.1%]).")  
- Setting: Commercial, Medicare, or Medicaid plans (¶4: "Other inclusion requirements were continuous enrollment in a commercial, Medicare, or Medicaid plan or dual Medicare-Medicaid eligibility for at least 12 months before (baseline) and at least 17 months after (follow-up) the index date.")

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcome: Prevalence of GLP-1 agonist discontinuation at 3, 6, and 12 months (¶7: "Overall prevalence of GLP-1 agonist discontinuation was 26.2%, 30.8%, and 36.5% at 3, 6, and 12 months, respectively ( Table 1 ).")  
- Secondary outcomes: Adherence (measured as proportion of days covered [PDC]) at 3, 6, and 12 months; factors associated with discontinuation (e.g., demographics, clinical conditions, financial factors) (¶5: "Adherence, defined as proportion of days covered (PDC), was calculated allowing 10 days, at most, between prescriptions. Patient demographic characteristics included age, sex, and race and ethnicity, among others. Baseline clinical characteristics were T2D, obesity, heart failure (HF), other CVD conditions besides HF, and chronic kidney disease. Adverse effects included new gastrointestinal disease diagnosis at the 12-month follow-up. Out-of-pocket (OOP) cost (2023 amounts) was calculated per 30-day supply of GLP-1 agonist at the 12-month follow-up.")  
- Follow-up time: 12 months (¶7: "Overall prevalence of GLP-1 agonist discontinuation was 26.2%, 30.8%, and 36.5% at 3, 6, and 12 months, respectively ( Table 1 ).")  
- Adverse events: New gastrointestinal disease diagnosis at 12-month follow-up (¶5: "Adverse effects included new gastrointestinal disease diagnosis at the 12-month follow-up.")

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Patients with obesity only had a higher prevalence of discontinuation at 12 months compared to those with T2D only or both (50.3% vs 35.8% and 34.2%) (¶7: "Patients with obesity only had a higher prevalence of discontinuation at 12 months vs those with T2D only and those with both (50.3% vs 35.8% and 34.2%).")  
- Discontinuation was associated with being Black or Hispanic, male, Medicare or Medicaid enrollees, living in areas with very high levels of social needs, having obesity only, heart failure, other CVD conditions besides HF, and new gastrointestinal adverse effects at follow-up (¶9: "Patients had significantly higher odds of discontinuation at 12 months if they were Black or Hispanic, male, and Medicare or Medicaid enrollees; lived in areas with very high levels of social needs; had obesity only, HF, or other CVD conditions besides HF at baseline; and had new gastrointestinal adverse effects at follow-up ( Table 2 ).")  
- Each 1-percentage point increase in out-of-pocket (OOP) cost per 30-day supply of GLP-1 agonist was associated with increased odds of discontinuation (odds ratio, 1.02; 95% CI, 1.02-1.03) (¶9: "Furthermore, each 1–percentage point increase in OOP cost per a 30-day supply of GLP-1 agonist was associated with increased odds of discontinuation (odds ratio, 1.02; 95% CI, 1.02-1.03) ( Table 2 ).")  

### Limitations stated by the authors
