### Relevance (1)
### P — Patient / 研究对象  
- Population: Adults with alcohol use disorder (AUD)  
- Inclusion criteria: Age 21 to 65 years, reporting past-year DSM-5 criteria for AUD, past-month endorsement of more than 7 (women) or more than 14 (men) standard drinks in a week with 2 or more heavy drinking episodes, ability to attend weekly clinic visits and pretreatment and posttreatment laboratory sessions  
- Exclusion criteria: Currently seeking treatment for alcohol problems or actively attempting to reduce consumption; past use of GLP-1 receptor agonists; weight loss medications; BMI less than 23; past-year substance use disorder other than AUD, tobacco use disorder, or mild cannabis use disorder; recent (30-day) use of illicit drugs except cannabis; history of diabetes, and current medical or neurological illness precluding participation based on physician judgement  
- Age/sex: Mean (SD) age 39.9 (10.6) years; 34 (71%) female, 14 (29%) male  
- Setting: Outpatient clinical and human laboratory components  
- Sample size: 48 participants randomized  

### I — Intervention / 干预措施  
- Intervention: Once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide  
- Dose: 0.25 mg/week for weeks 1-4, 0.5 mg/week for weeks 5-8, and 1.0 mg/week at week 9 (contingent on tolerability)  
- Frequency: Weekly  
- Route: Subcutaneous  
- Duration: 9 weeks of treatment  

### C — Comparator / 对照方式  
- Comparator: Placebo  
- Dose & duration: Placebo administered weekly for 9 weeks  

### O — Outcome / 结局指标  
- Primary outcomes:  
  - Grams of alcohol consumed (g-ETOH) during posttreatment laboratory self-administration (β, −0.48; 95% CI, −0.85 to −0.11; P = .01)  
  - Peak breath alcohol concentration (BrAC) during posttreatment laboratory self-administration (β, −0.46; 95% CI, −0.87 to −0.06; P = .03)  
- Secondary outcomes:  
  - Drinks per calendar day (β, −0.27; 95% CI, −0.63 to 0.09; P = .17)  
  - Drinks per drinking day (β, −0.41; 95% CI, −0.73 to −0.09; P = .04)  
  - Weekly craving (β, −0.39; 95% CI, −0.73 to −0.06; P = .01)  
  - Cigarettes per day (significant time-by-treatment interaction; β, −0.10; 95% CI, −0.16 to −0.03; P = .005)  
- Adverse events: No serious adverse events, adverse interactions with alcohol, or treatment-related discontinuations were recorded. Participants treated with semaglutide reported expected adverse effects, which were largely mild in severity  
- Follow-up time: 9 weeks of treatment with a final assessment visit at week 10  

### S — Study design / 研究设计  
- Study design: Phase 2 randomized clinical trial  
- Blinding: Participants, investigators, and outcome assessors were blind to condition  
- Randomization: Randomized to semaglutide or placebo  
- Number of centres: Conducted at the University of North Carolina (UNC)–Chapel Hill School of Medicine  
- Registration: Not explicitly mentioned  
- Follow-up duration: 9 weeks of treatment with a final assessment visit at week 10  

### Key findings relevant to the question  
- Semaglutide reduced posttreatment laboratory consumption with a medium to large effect size for grams of alcohol consumed (β, −0.48; 95% CI, −0.85 to −0.11; P = .01) and peak breath alcohol concentration (β, −0.46; 95% CI, −0.87 to −0.06; P = .03) (¶21: "Results of residualized change models indicated that semaglutide reduced posttreatment laboratory consumption with a medium to large effect size for grams of alcohol consumed (β, −0.48; 95% CI, −0.85 to −0.11; P = .01) and peak breath alcohol concentration (β, −0.46; 95% CI, −0.87 to −0.06; P = .03)")  
- Semaglutide significantly reduced weekly craving (β, −0.39; 95% CI, −0.73 to −0.06; P = .01) (¶23: "Semaglutide significantly reduced weekly craving (β, −0.39; 95% CI, −0.73 to −0.06; P = .01)")  
- The proportion of participants with zero heavy drinking days increased significantly in the semaglutide group across the 2 dose phases (¶25: "The likelihood of zero heavy drinking days increased significantly from weeks 1 through 4 to 5 through 8 in the semaglutide group (z score, −2.93; P = .003)")  
- Body weight (kg) change at discharge averaged −5% in the semaglutide group (mean [SD], −5.05% [3.56] vs 0.18% [2.5] in the placebo group; time-by-treatment interaction: β, −0.07; 95% CI, −0.08 to −0.05; P < .001) (¶27: "Body weight (kg) change at discharge averaged −5% in the semaglutide group (mean [SD], −5.05% [3.56] vs 0.18% [2.5] in the placebo group; time-by-treatment interaction: β, −0.07; 95% CI, −0.08 to −0.05; P < .001)")  

### Limitations stated by the authors  
- Modest sample size and short-term treatment duration, both reflecting the phase 2 stage of this trial (¶37: "Among several study limitations are the modest sample size and short-term treatment duration, both reflecting the phase 2 stage of this trial.")  
- The use of low-dose semaglutide to maximize safety and feasibility likely limited detection of significant effects (¶37: "The use of low-dose semaglutide to maximize safety and feasibility likely limited detection of significant effects.")  
- The moderate level of AUD severity of this sample, with consumption levels below those of most treatment-seeking samples (¶37: "Another limitation is the moderate level of AUD severity of this sample, with consumption levels below those of most treatment-seeking samples.")
